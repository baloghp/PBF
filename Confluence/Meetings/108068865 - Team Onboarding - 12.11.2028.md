## <custom data-type="emoji" data-id="id-12">:speaking_head:</custom> Discussion topics

**Key Points**   

*  Team introductions revealed diverse backgrounds with Leila Laham from Canada focusing on technical aspects, Chris Lee from the US specializing in organizational change management, Chris from Australia bringing IT healthcare project management experience, and Kristin Daniel from Texas contributing contractor-side PBP expertise.
* Oliver Lehmann explained his vision for the Contract of the Year award as a tool to achieve recognition and acceptance of project business management as a core discipline rather than a fringe aspect of project management.
* Concept is found in the concept pages of Confluence: <custom data-type="smartlink" data-id="id-13">https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/97419265</custom>  Quick takeaways:

    * Multiple categories will be established based on contractor maturity levels including startups, established organizations, and major organizations to ensure fair competition across different playing fields.
    * A two-phase evaluation approach was proposed following PMI's model with written submissions in phase one and virtual presentations by finalists in phase two.
    * Eligibility criteria were defined requiring independent contractor-customer projects completed in 2024-2025 or currently running, with paying customers under contract
    * Evaluation framework criteria were outlined including project success, agility, commercial model, legal soundness, interface governance, risk management, team and business acumen.
    

## <custom data-type="emoji" data-id="id-14">:brain:</custom>  Brainstorming / Ideas

* <custom data-type="mention" data-id="id-15">@Leila Lahham</custom> Leila raised concerns about the client assessment form requirement, noting potential risks in getting customers to complete evaluations after project completion due to lack of control over customer responsiveness.

    * Peter proposed a QR code solution for client assessments where nominees provide codes to clients for quick five-minute evaluations, addressing the customer participation challenge.
    
* <custom data-type="mention" data-id="id-16">@Kris Lea</custom>  questioned whether the jury would consist of different people than the current team, raising concerns about external validation and conflict of interest management.

    * Oliver suggested involving big names in the jury for additional visibility while acknowledging the trade-off between promotional benefits and additional coordination effort.
    
* 
* Project timeline was confirmed with concept development in November-December, submissions in January-February, and jury evaluation with results announcement in March to align with Q1 business cycles.
