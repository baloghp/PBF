## Discussion Notes (to capture during meeting)

* Three project categories were established based on headcount: projects are categorized by the number of participants working on the customer project, excluding internal administration or bookkeeping staff.
* Budget requirements were modified to focus on people development investment rather than profit disclosure, specifically asking what percentage of net income goes toward developing staff who worked on the project.
* Publication strategy was decided with content appearing on both Peter's Wix platform and Oliver's WordPress site, as Peter can perform necessary development work more easily on the Wix platform.
* Data storage will be handled through European data centers in Ireland via Amazon Web Services, with both Peter's and Oliver's accounts operating under GDPR compliance with one-year retention.
* <custom data-type="mention" data-id="id-7">@Christopher Kelly</custom> volunteered to provide a list of security requirements including penetration testing and infrastructure auditing standards for the data hosting platform.
* Pilot testing phase was agreed upon where team members will role-play as clients submitting test applications to identify issues and refine the process before going live.
* Four key roles were defined requiring detailed guides: nominee coach, assessor, judging panel member, and communications coordinator, with team members volunteering to develop specific guides.
* Submission package was enhanced with three new quality assessment areas: project exemplarity and innovation, impact measurement using the STAR method, and industry contribution through knowledge sharing.
* A support email system will be established for assessors and judges to ask questions, with responses handled by the core team within 48 hours rather than assigning individual contacts.
* Award credo was developed around five "beyond" principles: beyond competence to excellence, beyond compliance to impact, beyond project success to industry contribution, beyond standard practice to innovation, and beyond completion to legacy. Integrity is to be worked into the credo.
* Platform development will use Wix for the main website and Oliver's Survey Monkey account for forms, with Oliver arranging for professional logo design and digital badge creation through Credibly.
* Timeline flexibility was established with quality taking priority over the original March deadline, potentially extending completion to April to ensure proper pilot testing and refinement.
* Next meeting was rescheduled from December 25th to January 7th to avoid the Christmas holiday, with team members continuing work on assigned guides during the break.
