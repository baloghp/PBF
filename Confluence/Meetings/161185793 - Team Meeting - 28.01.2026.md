## Context

* **Last meeting (07.01.2026):** Pilot project selected, roles for internal role-play assigned, and January role-play timeline outlined.
* **Since last meeting:** Award homepage designed and reviewed; technical work on platform continues. Need to streamline how we run future meetings so Peter can focus on technical development.

## Goals

* Review and gather feedback on the **Award Homepage** (structure, copy, user journey).
* Agree who will **own future meeting agendas and facilitation** (so Peter can focus on technical work).
* Clarify what **Oliver's feedback and approvals** we need (homepage and communications) and align on how to get them, including the right email contact.

## Agenda (detailed topics)

### 1. Check-in & recap (5 min)

* Quick round: any blockers or urgent updates since last meeting?
* Confirm today's focus: homepage review, meeting facilitation support, Oliver inputs.

### 2. Award Homepage Walkthrough & Feedback (25 min)

* **Demo / walkthrough**

    * Visit the live/staging Award homepage together.
    * Walk through:
    
        * Overall structure and navigation
        * Nominee and client journeys (what they see first, how they start)
        * Key messages (value proposition, credibility, "why this award")
        
    
* **Feedback & decisions**

    * What works well? What feels confusing or missing?
    * Are the calls-to-action clear enough?
    * Are there any blockers to using this page for the pilot?
    * **Decide:** 
    
        * \[ \] Is this page "good enough" for pilot/internal use?
        * \[ \] List of small tweaks vs. bigger changes (and owners).
        
    

### 3. Support for Future Meeting Management (15 min)

* **Context (Peter):**

    * Needs to prioritize technical development (platform, implementation).
    * Meeting prep and agenda-writing are taking too much bandwidth.
    
* **Ask to volunteers:**

    * Identify 1–2 volunteers to:
    
        * Draft and maintain meeting agendas.
        * Coordinate timeboxes and facilitation during meetings.
        * Capture and clean up action items after meetings.
        
    
* **Decide & document:**

    * \[ \] Primary "Meeting Facilitator / Agenda Owner" (name).
    * \[ \] Backup person (name, optional).
    * \[ \] Simple workflow for:
    
        * Proposing agenda items.
        * Finalizing and sharing the agenda before each meeting.
        * Capturing actions and decisions.
        
    

### 4. Oliver's Feedback & Communication Channel (10–15 min)

* **What we need from Oliver now**

    * Homepage:
    
        * Visual/design sign-off?
        * Copy / messaging approvals?
        * Any constraints around use of Credibly branding or language?
        
    * Communications:
    
        * Approval for how we describe the award publicly.
        * Confirmation on support email approach (central address, who monitors it).
        
    
* **Email / contact details**

    * Confirm the **best email address** and format for:
    
        * Homepage-related approvals.
        * Ongoing communications about the award (team ↔ Oliver).
        
    * Draft short bullet list of questions/asks to send Oliver after this meeting.
    
* **Decide:**

    * \[ \] Who sends the summary email to Oliver.
    * \[ \] Deadline for getting his feedback (date).
