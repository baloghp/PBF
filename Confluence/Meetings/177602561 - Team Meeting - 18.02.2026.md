## 1. Technical Implementation Status & Demo

Peter Balogh demonstrated the current state of the system, focusing on the core workflows developed with Leila.

* **Authentication & Login**:

    * The authentication flow is complete, including a sign-up/login form.
    * Users can log in using Google credentials (preferred for ease of use).
    * Once authenticated, users are automatically redirected to their role-based dashboard (e.g., nominee, assessor, judge, or customer).
    
        

    
    
* **Nomination Submission Flow**:

    * **Fields**: Includes project name, company name (updated to "Contractor Company" for clarity), and three narrative text boxes (6,000 characters each) for project excellence, impact, and lessons learned.
    * **File Uploads**: Supports three PDF uploads (Main Narrative, Contract Matrix, RACI Matrix) with a 2MB size limit per file.
    * **Drafting**: Nominees can save progress as a draft or submit it as a final nomination.
    * **Locking**: Once submitted, the nomination becomes "read-only." The team discussed whether to allow re-opening for edits based on coach feedback but leaned toward locking it upon submission.
    
        

    
    
* **Customer Validation Portal**:

    * A separate page for customers to provide feedback using a scoring system (radio buttons) for categories like communication, HR, and safety protocols.
    * A fix is currently being worked on for a display issue where radio buttons were linked to the database but not visible.
    
        

    
    

## 2. Key Decisions Made

* **User Interface**:

    * The term "Company" on the nomination form will be changed to **"Contractor Company"** to avoid confusion for client-led nominations.
    * The **scoring radio buttons** will be redesigned for better clarity (e.g., placing numbers above/below buttons and increasing spacing).
    * The "0" score option may be removed from the scale.
    * Decision is made that volunteers of UAT testing will be also accepted a nominees in the award proceedings.
    
        

    
    
* **Administrative Updates**:

    * **Logo**: The new logo design was officially agreed upon following a previous informal meeting.
    * **Leadership**: Due to a new job and startup commitments, Peter may request other team members (like Chris) to lead future meetings.

## Technical progress (implementation)

### Main page, login & role-based dashboards

* **Main page** includes a **login and authentication workflow**.
* After login, each member is routed to their **dedicated dashboard page based on their role** (e.g. nominee coach, assessor, judge, customer).
* Role-based access ensures users only see the dashboards and actions relevant to their role.

### Nomination submission (in scope for demo)

* **Nomination submission flow** is in place with:

    * **Save draft** – nominees can save progress and return later.
    * **Validation before submission** – checks run before the nomination is submitted.
    * **File uploads** – support for uploading files as part of the nomination.
    
* **Not yet in scope:** Adding customer details (e.g. customer validation / client assessment flow) will be missing in the current build and is planned for later.
