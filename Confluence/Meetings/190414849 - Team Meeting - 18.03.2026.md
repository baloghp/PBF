### 2. Demo — Coach Dashboard, COI & Coach Diary (Peter) (15–20 min)

* Walkthrough using a sample nomination:

    * Coach Dashboard: view assigned nominations, statuses, and key indicators.
    * Nomination detail: record/view **COI** for coach if applicable.
    * **Coach Diary**:
    
        * Show the default template (eligibility & completeness checklists, assignment, COI recusals, progress, moderation, final score & shortlisting, notification).
        * Demonstrate how coaches update the diary during Stage 1.
        
    * How completeness and eligibility are now **coach responsibilities** (no separate assessor step).
    
* Decisions / questions:

    * Is anything missing from the Coach Diary template for this iteration?
    * Do we need any extra structured fields beyond the diary and simple flags (e.g. "Ready for assessment")?
    

### 3. Demo — Assessor Dashboard & Stage 1 assessor form (Peter) (20–25 min)

* Assessor Dashboard:

    * View assigned nominations, deadlines, and status.
    * Drill into nomination to see **read-only packet** and customer evaluation summary.
    
* **COI handling for assessors**:

    * Single "No COI" checkbox and **COI popup** explaining what a conflict is and the instruction to contact organisers via the registration channel.
    
* **Stage 1 assessor form** (based on `Stage-1-Assessor-Form-Spec.md`):

    * Structure: Acknowledge assignment → COI → 9 criteria scoring (0–5) → per-criterion comments → review & submit.
    * Criteria and scale (0–5) aligned with Evaluation Framework; highlight **Criterion 9 — Innovation & Industry Advancement** as critical.
    
* Decisions / questions:

    * Confirm any **weights** per criterion and how the **Client Assessment** score feeds into the overall Stage 1 score.
    * Should assessors see detailed customer scores or only a summary while scoring?
    * Any UX changes needed before UAT (e.g. wording, field labels, validation)?
    

### 4. Overall scoring & statistics / leaderboard dashboard (10–15 min)

* Present planned logic for **overall Stage 1 scoring**:

    * Combination of assessor scores (with moderation) and client assessment score.
    * How final Stage 1 scores are stored and surfaced to coaches/admins.
    
* Outline the proposed **statistics dashboard**:

    * Number of nominations by category and status (Submitted / Under Review / Shortlisted / Not Shortlisted).
    * Basic **leaderboard**: top nominations per category by Stage 1 score (and possibly Criterion 9 score).
    
* Decisions / questions:

    * Minimum statistics & leaderboard features needed for UAT vs later.
    * Any privacy / access rules (who can see rankings and scores).
    

### 5. UAT plan — scope, testers, and logistics (15–20 min)

* **Scope & objectives**:

    * Which flows are in-scope for UAT (nominee, coach, assessor, customer, comms)?
    * What we want to learn from UAT (usability, clarity of guidance, correctness of flows, performance).
    
* **Participants & roles**:

    * Target numbers per role (nominees, coaches, assessors) based on Kris + Oliver's outreach.
    * How we assign roles in Wix (Site members) and grant access.
    
* **Test scripts & feedback collection**:

    * Do we use role-based **UAT scripts** / checklists per role?
    * How testers will report issues (Discord channels, Jira, Confluence page, or form).
    * Where we track UAT findings and decisions.
    
* **Timelines**:

    * Proposed UAT start date, end date, and buffer for fixes.
    * Check alignment with team availability.
