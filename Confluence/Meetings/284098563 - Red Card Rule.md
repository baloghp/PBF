## Purpose

Make sure nothing important gets steamrolled. Modeled on the Toyota **andon cord**: anyone can stop the line; stopping is a service to the team.

## The rule

1. **Anyone** may play a red card — say "red card" or post 🟥. No justification needed to play it.
2. **We stop immediately.** Current speaker finishes their sentence; floor to the card holder.
3. Holder gets **up to 1 minute, uninterrupted**, to state what isn't right.
4. Chair leads resolution, **≤5 minutes**. Holder confirms:

    * 🟢 **Resolved now**
    * 👉 **Parked & assigned** (owner + date in minutes)

5. **Do not move on** until the holder confirms 🟢 or 👉. Holder decides, not the chair.
6. Red cards are never counted or judged negatively.
