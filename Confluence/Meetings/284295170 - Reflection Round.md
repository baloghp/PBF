## Purpose

End every meeting with each voice heard. Surface friction while it is small. About _how we worked together_, not the topics.

## The format

1. Chair reserves the **last 5 minutes** — protected; cut the agenda, not the reflection.
2. **Everyone speaks**, one at a time, **≤30 seconds** each.
3. Same two prompts: ➕ one thing that worked · 🔄 one thing to change. "Pass" allowed.
4. **Received, not debated.** Only reply: "thank you."
5. **Chair speaks last.**
6. Note-taker captures **🔄 themes only** (no attributed quotes). A 🔄 twice in a row → next agenda.
