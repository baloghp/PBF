## 1. Model

Volunteer-run programme — **no cash sponsorship** in cycle 1. Partners contribute **goods, services, or direct payment to vendors** (not to the core team) for **defined recognition**.

| In-kind | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Amplification | LinkedIn posts, newsletter, internal nomination drive |
| Prizes | Product, service, training, subscription for winners/finalists |
| Ceremony | Flights, hotel, venue, catering, livestream — **paid direct to vendor** |
| Operations | Design, printing, software, pro bono legal |

**Not in scope:** cash grants or reimbursements to individuals · sponsors influencing nominations/assessment/winners · product endorsement (use **Supported by** / **Prize courtesy of** only).

**Cycle context (DR-001):** nominations **1 Sep – 31 Oct 2026** · finalists **30 Nov** · winners **Feb 2027** (virtual default unless Tier 1 enables in-person).

## 2. Tiers (ranked by probable investment)

Tiers **1–3** include the **standard amplifier package** (§3). Higher tiers add ceremony and/or prize on top.

| Rank | Tier | Slots | Partner gives | PCOTY gives |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **Founding partner** | **1** | Ceremony scope in Schedule A (travel/venue/AV **direct to vendors**) + §3 amplifier · optional category prize | §3 amplifier · top logo · **Ceremony supported by [Partner]** · optional **Prize courtesy of** · ceremony mention · category exclusivity if agreed · wrap report |
| **2** | **Prize partner** | **3-6-9** (S/M/L) | Defined category **prize** (winner or finalists — agree upfront) · delivery within **30 days** of Feb 2027 · §3 amplifier | §3 amplifier · **Prize courtesy of [Partner]** on site · category in winner campaign · ceremony thank-you · **one partner per category** |
| **3** | **Amplification partner** | **Open** | §3 amplifier only | §3 amplifier · **Amplification partner** label · optional tool-category exclusivity (case by case) |
| **4** | **Community supporter** | Open | **One** LinkedIn repost **or** one internal nomination nudge (Sep–Oct) | Name on Supporters list · Showcase thank-you (no logo) |
| **5** | **Services partner** *(optional)* | As needed | Scoped SOW (design, legal, software, printing) | **Services supported by [Partner]** where visible · mention if partner agrees · *no §3 unless combined with Tier 3+* |

**Ranking:** ceremony/hospitality > prize > amplification > community. Tier 5 is orthogonal (can stack with Tier 3).

**Warm leads:** Oliver's network, core team contacts, UAT volunteers' employers (with permission). Segments: PM tools (2–3), contractors (3), training (2–3), bodies/media (3–4), travel/hospitality (1).

## 4. Governance & agreement

Governance rules (non-negotiable) and **partnership confirmation** templates → **[Partnership legalities & confirmation](https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/263520298)**.

**Non-negotiable** (adapted from [Business Care Award rules](https://businesscareaward.com/en/award-rules.html)):

- Partners **must not** influence intake, assessor assignment, scoring, shortlist, or winners.
- Partner support **not required** to nominate, assess, or win.
- No partner on **judging panel** with voting rights.
- **Partnership confirmation + Schedule A** (Tiers 1–3) before public use of name/logo; PCOTY may decline COI or brand conflicts (WS5 / Programme Owner).
