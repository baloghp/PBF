## 1. Messaging strategy (by tier)

| Tier | Who | Core pitch | Lead with | Ask | Don’t say |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1 Founding** | Airlines, hotels, venues, AV, conference brands | “Enable the **first live** winner ceremony — visible legacy partner” | First post-UAT cycle; Oliver Lehmann; 16-volunteer UAT done | Book travel/venue/AV **direct to vendor** + amplifier package | “Sponsor us” / cash / “official PBF contract” |
| **2 Prize** | PM tools, training, consultancies | “Put your product/service in front of **winners and finalists** in your category” | Category fit (Small/Medium/Large); fair value prize | Defined prize to **winner** (or finalists) + amplifier | Open-ended “support the award” |
| **3 Amplification** | Tools, contractors, media, bodies | “Reach project leaders during **nomination season** — mutual LinkedIn lift” | Sep–Oct nomination window; Showcase audience | Full **amplifier package** (2–3 posts) | Exclusivity unless agreed |
| **4 Community** | Alumni, small firms, volunteers’ employers | “Low-friction way to back the community” | One repost or internal nudge | Single touch in Sep–Oct | Logo block / heavy commitment |
| **5 Services** | Design, legal, software, print | “Pro bono scoped help — credited where visible” | Specific deliverable + deadline | SOW with named output | Open-ended consulting |

**Shared messages (all tiers)**

- Recognises **contractor–client project excellence** (Project Business), not generic “PM of the year”.
- **Nominations 1 Sep – 31 Oct 2026** → winners **Feb 2027**.
- Partners **never** influence who wins; support is **not required** to nominate or win.
- Wording: **Supported by** / **Prize courtesy of** / **Ceremony supported by** — not product endorsement.
- Documentation (confirmation, email, SOW) → [Partnership legalities & confirmation](https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/263520298).
