## 1. Do we need a contract?

**Short answer: no formal contract in cycle 1** — and you should **not** present PBF/PCOTY as a contracting party if PBF is not a registered legal entity.

| What partners might expect | What we do instead |
| --- | --- |
| Sponsorship **contract** | **Partnership confirmation** (1–2 pages) — mutual deliverables, governance, logo use |
| Payment to PBF | **No cash** — partner pays **vendors or winners direct** |
| PBF as legal counterparty | **Programme team** acts on behalf of the award; signatory is **Programme Owner** (Oliver F. Lehmann) with team mandate |
| Lawyer-drafted agreement | **Lightweight template** below; optional pro bono review (Tier 5 legal partner) |

**Be transparent in every conversation:**

> The Project Contractor of the Year Award is a **volunteer-run programme** presented by the **Project Business Foundation** (mission and brand). We are **not** soliciting cash sponsorship. In-kind support is documented in a **partnership confirmation** so both sides know what was agreed — not a commercial contract.

**When written confirmation is required**

| Tier | Minimum documentation |
| --- | --- |
| **1–3** | Signed **Partnership confirmation + Schedule A** before public use of name/logo |
| **4** | **Email confirmation** (one thread) with tier + single action + cycle dates — no signature needed |
| **5** | **SOW** scoped to deliverable (design file, legal review memo) — partner ↔ named programme contact |

**Open for 15 Jul:** Confirm with Oliver whether PBF has *any* registered form (e.g. Verein, association). If **none**, all materials say **“PCOTY programme team”** / **“presented by Project Business Foundation”** — never imply PBF can sue or be sued as a party.

## 2. Messaging strategy (by tier)

| Tier | Who | Core pitch | Lead with | Ask | Don’t say |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1 Founding** | Airlines, hotels, venues, AV, conference brands | “Enable the **first live** winner ceremony — visible legacy partner” | First post-UAT cycle; Oliver Lehmann; 16-volunteer UAT done | Book travel/venue/AV **direct to vendor** + amplifier package | “Sponsor us” / cash / “official PBF contract” |
| **2 Prize** | PM tools, training, consultancies | “Put your product/service in front of **winners and finalists** in your category” | Category fit (Small/Medium/Large); fair value prize | Defined prize to **winner** (or finalists) + amplifier | Open-ended “support the award” |
| **3 Amplification** | Tools, contractors, media, bodies | “Reach project leaders during **nomination season** — mutual LinkedIn lift” | Sep–Oct nomination window; Showcase audience | Full **amplifier package** (2–3 posts) | Exclusivity unless agreed |
| **4 Community** | Alumni, small firms, volunteers’ employers | “Low-friction way to back the community” | One repost or internal nudge | Single touch in Sep–Oct | Logo block / heavy commitment |
| **5 Services** | Design, legal, software, print | “Pro bono scoped help — credited where visible” | Specific deliverable + deadline | SOW with named output | Open-ended consulting |

**Shared messages (all tiers)**

- Recognises **contractor–client project excellence** (Project Business), not generic “PM of the year”.
- **Nominations 1 Sep – 31 Oct 2026** → winners **Feb 2027**.
- Partners **never** influence who wins; support is **not required** to nominate or win.
- Wording: **Supported by** / **Prize courtesy of** / **Ceremony supported by** — not product endorsement.

- **Partner:** [Legal name of partner organisation], represented by [name, title]

**Partner commitments (Schedule A).** [Tier] — attach: deliverables, dates, fair-value estimate (informational), prize spec or ceremony scope as applicable.

**Governance.**

- Partner will not influence nomination intake, assessment, shortlist, or winner selection.
- Partner support is not required to nominate, assess, or win.
- No partner representative on the judging panel with voting rights.

**Logo and name.** Mutual permission to use names/logos only as described in Schedule A and approved assets. Partner discloses the relationship on its own promotional posts.

**Prizes and vendors.** Where applicable, partner delivers prizes **directly to winners** or pays **suppliers directly** — not through programme volunteers personally.

**Term.** From signature until completion of Schedule A (expected Feb 2027 wrap report). Either party may withdraw on written notice if the other materially misses agreed deliverables.

**No legal entity representation.** Partner acknowledges the award is operated by a **volunteer programme team**. This confirmation records mutual intent and deliverables; it is **not** a substitute for legal advice. Partner should rely on its own counsel for tax, employment, or regulatory questions.
