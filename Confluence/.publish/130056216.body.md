## Overview

The Communications Coordinator handles all centralized communications and publications. This role **owns nominee notification templates and sends operational messages directly** (no Nominee Coach intermediary).

---

## Key Responsibilities

### Communication Strategy

* Coordinate overall communication strategy
* Maintain nominee notification templates and send directly to applicants
* Ensure consistent messaging across all communications

### Award Publication (3.2)

* Generate award letters/certificates
* Prepare publication materials (case stories, press releases, social media content)
* Publish award results on ITTD website and PBF website
* Handle post-award activities

### Support to Award Operations / nominees

* Provide notification templates
* Support with embargo management
* Coordinate publication materials for winners/finalists

## Process Phases

### Pre-Program Setup

**Before nominations open:**

1. **Develop communication strategy:**

    * Define key messages
    * Establish brand voice and tone
    * Create style guide
    * Plan communication channels
    
2. **Create notification templates:**

    * Submission confirmation
    * Completeness check notification
    * Shortlist notification
    * Finalist notification
    * Winner notification
    * Non-shortlisted notification
    * Non-finalist notification
    
3. **Set up communication infrastructure:**

    * Email templates in system
    * Platform notification settings
    * Embargo management process
    * Publication consent tracking
    
4. **Prepare publication materials:**

    * Award logo and branding
    * Website content templates
    * Social media templates
    * Press release template
    
5. **Coordinate with stakeholders:**

    * ITTD website contact
    * PBF website contact
    * Social media accounts
    * Press contacts (if applicable)

### Submission Phase

**Support nominees (Comms owns templates & sends):**

1. **Provide communication templates:**

    * Welcome email template
    * Missing items notification template
    * Submission confirmation template
    * Status update templates
    
2. **Ensure consistent messaging:**

    * Provide guidance on tone and content
    * Maintain brand consistency
    
3. **Monitor communication quality:**

    * Review sample communications
    * Provide feedback to Project Manager on tone and content
    * Update templates based on feedback
    

**Key deliverables:**

* All templates available; Comms sends directly to nominees
* Style guide distributed
* Communication guidelines documented

#### Step 2: Generate Award Letters/Certificates

1. **Create award letter/certificate:**

    * Include winner name/organization
    * Include award category
    * Include award year
    * Include recognition statement
    * Use official branding/logo
    
2. **Obtain approvals:**

    * Programme Owner signature
    * Final approval before sending
    
3. **Prepare for delivery:**

    * Digital version (PDF)
    * Physical version (if applicable)

#### Step 3: Prepare Publication Materials

**Case Stories:**

1. **Contact winners and top 3 finalists:**

    * Request additional information if needed
    * Schedule interview/call if needed
    * Gather photos/artifacts (if applicable)
    
2. **Write case stories:**

    * **Contractor of the Year winner:** Full case story (500-800 words)
    * **Top 3 finalists:** Shorter case stories (300-500 words each)
    * Focus on:
    
        * What made them exemplary
        * Innovation and uniqueness
        * Measurable impact
        * Industry contribution
        * Lessons learned
        
    
3. **Review with nominees:**

    * Share draft for review
    * Incorporate feedback
    * Obtain final approval
    
4. **Obtain final publication consents:**

    * Confirm consent for case story publication
    * Confirm consent for photos (if applicable)
    * Document consents
    

**Press Releases:**

1. **Write press release:**

    * Announce winners
    * Highlight key achievements
    * Include quotes (if available)
    * Include award program information
    
2. **Review and approve:**

    * Programme Owner review
    * Final approval
    
3. **Prepare distribution list:**

    * Relevant industry publications
    * Professional associations
    * Media contacts (if applicable)
    

**Social Media Content:**

1. **Create social media posts:**

    * Winner announcement post
    * Individual winner posts (if applicable)
    * Finalist recognition posts
    * Behind-the-scenes content (if applicable)
    
2. **Prepare visuals:**

    * Winner photos/logos
    * Award graphics
    * Infographics (if applicable)
    
3. **Schedule posts:**

    * Coordinate timing with embargo lift
    * Plan posting schedule
    * Prepare responses to comments
    

**Ceremony Materials (if applicable):**

1. **Plan ceremony format:**

    * Virtual or in-person
    * Agenda and timeline
    * Presentation materials
    
2. **Prepare materials:**

    * Winner presentations
    * Award ceremony script
    * Visual materials
    * Recording setup (if virtual)
    

#### Step 4: Embargo Management

**Embargo Procedures:**

1. **Set embargo period:**

    * Define embargo start date (when winners notified)
    * Define embargo end date (official announcement date)
    * Communicate embargo clearly to all recipients
    
2. **Embargo communications:**

    * Include embargo notice in all winner notifications
    * Explain embargo terms clearly
    * Specify consequences of breaking embargo
    
3. **Monitor embargo compliance:**

    * Track any embargo breaches
    * Address breaches immediately
    * Document any issues
    
4. **Lift embargo:**

    * Coordinate embargo lift timing
    * Notify all stakeholders
    * Release all materials simultaneously
    

**Embargo Notice Template:**

```
IMPORTANT: EMBARGO NOTICE

This notification is under EMBARGO until [Date] at [Time] [Timezone].

You must NOT:
- Share this news publicly (social media, press, website, etc.)
- Announce this to your team or stakeholders
- Issue any press releases or communications

You MAY:
- Share with immediate family/close advisors (under confidentiality)
- Prepare internal communications (to be released after embargo)

The embargo will be lifted on [Date] when we make the official public announcement.

If you have questions about the embargo, please contact [Communications Coordinator] immediately.

Thank you for your cooperation.
```

## Publication Guidelines

### Embargo Procedures

**Purpose:** To ensure coordinated, professional announcement of winners

**Embargo Period:**

* Starts: When winners are notified
* Ends: Official public announcement date/time
* Duration: Typically 7-14 days (to be determined by project)

**Embargo Rules:**

1. **Winners must NOT:**

    * Share news publicly (social media, press, website)
    * Announce to team/stakeholders
    * Issue press releases
    
2. **Winners MAY:**

    * Share with immediate family/close advisors (under confidentiality)
    * Prepare internal communications (to be released after embargo)
    
3. **Enforcement:**

    * Clear communication of embargo terms
    * Monitoring for breaches
    * Immediate response to breaches
    * Potential consequences (to be defined)
    

### Publication Consent Management

**Consent Tracking:**

1. **Initial consent:**

    * Obtained during submission
    * Documented in platform
    * Covers: Evaluation, communications, short story publication
    
2. **Final consent:**

    * Confirmed before publication
    * Covers: Case story, photos, quotes
    * Documented separately
    
3. **Consent management:**

    * Track all consents
    * Verify before publication
    * Document any withdrawals
    

### Website Publication Procedures

**ITTD Website:**

1. **Content preparation:**

    * Frame as cooperation with PBF
    * Include winner announcements
    * Include case stories
    * Include methodology summary
    
2. **Coordination:**

    * Contact ITTD website administrator
    * Provide all materials
    * Coordinate publication timing
    * Review published content
    

**PBF Website:**

1. **Content preparation:**

    * Frame as cooperation with ITTD
    * Include winner announcements
    * Include case stories
    * Include methodology summary
    
2. **Coordination:**

    * Contact PBF website administrator
    * Provide all materials
    * Coordinate publication timing
    * Review published content
    

### Social Media Guidelines

**Content Strategy:**

1. **Tone:**

    * Professional yet celebratory
    * Focus on achievements
    * Highlight innovation and impact
    
2. **Content types:**

    * Winner announcements
    * Case story highlights
    * Behind-the-scenes (if appropriate)
    * Industry insights
    
3. **Timing:**

    * Coordinate with embargo lift
    * Stagger posts for maximum engagement
    * Respond to comments promptly
    
4. **Visuals:**

    * Use official branding
    * Include winner photos/logos
    * Create engaging graphics

## Common Issues & Solutions

### Issue: Winner breaks embargo

**Solution:**

1. Contact winner immediately
2. Assess impact
3. Decide on response (may need to lift embargo early)
4. Document incident
5. Review embargo communication for future

### Issue: Publication consent not obtained

**Solution:**

1. Contact nominee immediately
2. Obtain consent before publication
3. If consent not given, remove from publication
4. Document decision

### Issue: Case story needs significant revision

**Solution:**

1. Review feedback with nominee
2. Revise case story
3. Obtain approval
4. Update publication timeline if needed

### Issue: Website publication delayed

**Solution:**

1. Contact website administrator
2. Identify cause of delay
3. Adjust timeline if needed
4. Communicate delay to stakeholders

### Issue: Negative feedback or complaints

**Solution:**

1. Acknowledge feedback
2. Investigate issue
3. Respond professionally
4. Document and learn
5. Update processes if needed
