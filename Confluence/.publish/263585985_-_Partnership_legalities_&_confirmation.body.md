## 1. Do we need a contract?

**No formal sponsorship contract in cycle 1** — especially if PBF is **not a registered legal entity**.

| What partners might expect | What we do instead |
| --- | --- |
| Sponsorship **contract** | **Partnership confirmation** (1–2 pages) — mutual deliverables, governance, logo use |
| Payment to PBF | **No cash** — partner pays **vendors or winners direct** |
| PBF as legal counterparty | **Programme team** on behalf of the award; signatory **Oliver F. Lehmann**, Programme Owner |
| Lawyer-drafted agreement | Templates below; partner uses **own counsel** for tax/compliance questions |

**Say this in conversations:**

> The award is a **volunteer-run programme** presented by the **Project Business Foundation** (mission and brand). We don't take cash sponsorship. In-kind support is documented in a **partnership confirmation** so both sides know what was agreed — not a commercial contract.

## 4. Governance clauses *(include in every confirmation)*

- Partner **must not** influence nomination intake, assessor assignment, scoring, shortlist, or winner selection.
- Partner support is **not required** to nominate, assess, or win.
- No partner representative on the **judging panel** with voting rights.
- PCOTY may decline partners whose brand conflicts with programme values or creates COI (WS5 / Programme Owner).

Full award governance: [Governance & Jury Protocol](https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/97779722).

**Project Contractor of the Year Award** — cycle 2026 (winners announced February 2027)

   Represented by: **Oliver F. Lehmann**, Programme Owner  
   Contact: [email]

   Represented by: [Name], [Title]  
   Contact: [email]

---

**1. Purpose**

This confirmation documents **in-kind** support and mutual recognition for the P-CotY 2026 cycle. It is **not** a cash sponsorship agreement. Neither party transfers funds to the other under this confirmation.

**2. Partner commitments**

As set out in **Schedule A** attached:

- Tier: [Founding / Prize / Amplification partner]
- In-kind deliverables: [summary]
- Estimated fair value (informational only): [€X or description]

**3. Programme commitments**

Recognition as defined in Schedule A and the [In-kind partners — cycle 1](https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/263520270) tier table (logo placement, Showcase posts, ceremony mention, wrap report as applicable).

**4. Governance**

The Partner agrees that:

(a) it will not influence nomination intake, assessment, shortlisting, or winner selection;  
(b) its support is not required for any party to nominate, assess, or win;  
(c) no representative of the Partner will sit on the judging panel with voting rights.

**5. Logo and name**

Each party grants the other permission to use its name and logo only as described in Schedule A and approved brand assets. The Partner will disclose its support relationship on its own promotional posts (e.g. `#ad`, “Proud supporter of…”).

**6. Prizes and vendors**

Where Schedule A includes prizes or ceremony support, the Partner will deliver prizes **directly to winners** or pay **suppliers directly**. No payment shall be made to individual programme volunteers.

**7. Term**

From the date of signing until completion of Schedule A deliverables (expected February 2027). Either party may withdraw on **14 days' written notice** if the other materially fails to meet agreed deliverables.

**8. No legal entity representation**

The Partner acknowledges that the Award is operated by a **volunteer programme team**. This confirmation records mutual intent and deliverables. It is **not legal advice** and **not** a substitute for counsel. Each party should consult its own advisors on tax, employment, regulatory, or compliance matters.
