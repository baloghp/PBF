**Page 1** – The Award and About the award.

Consider adding the year for this award ie 2026. Include that this is the inaugural year to generate appetite for applicants to be the first to win the award. Consider emphasising what makes this award different (standout) from the rest.  PBF's core message as I see it, "In Project Business, projects are no longer just done to support a business. They _are_ the business."  I believe the award should highlight the commercial, contractual, and cross‑corporate nature of project business.

I ran it through Co-Pilot with relevant prompts to have this for consideration

"**The Project Business Foundation (PBF) Contractor of the Year Award honors outstanding contractor–client projects that exemplify excellence in _Project Business_, where projects are not just work, but the business itself.**

This award recognizes contractors who have delivered exceptional cross‑corporate outcomes:

* creating measurable business value for customers,
* demonstrating commercial and contractual excellence,
* fostering trust and collaboration across multi‑party project ecosystems, and
* advancing the discipline of Project Business Management through innovation and shared learning.

Whether you are an independent professional or part of a global enterprise, if your project within the last two years demonstrates excellence beyond standard practice, commercial impact beyond contractual compliance, and a legacy that strengthens both your business and the wider project business community, we invite you to share your story.

Award recipients join a global network of leaders shaping the future of cross‑corporate project delivery and contributing to the evolution of Project Business as a profession"
