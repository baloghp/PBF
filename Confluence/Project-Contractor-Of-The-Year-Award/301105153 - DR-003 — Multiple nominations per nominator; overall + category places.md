## Decision

Two rules for the live P-CotY 2026 cycle (and until a later DR supersedes them):

1. **One nominator may submit multiple nominations.** Same account, more than one packet. Each nomination is a **distinct project**, with its own narrative, evidence, and client evaluation. This is allowed so one person can put more than one piece of work on the table and multiply their chances.
2. **Winner set.** One **overall** Project Contractor of the Year, **plus three recognised places in each category** (Small, Medium, Large). Categories are **assigned at intake** from customer-project headcount and used for Stage 1 ranking. Stage 2 **names** the places and the overall winner. It does **not** assign categories.

## Context

The public CTA and some UAT-era copy treated a nominator as having a single nomination. That blocks a strong contractor (or a nominator putting several contractors forward) from entering more than one project.

Winner language was still soft: category winners, an overall title "if applicable", and "top-3 finalists" without a locked set. Staging of categories was already intake in WS4 / Overview; this record **rejects** any model that leaves category assignment until the judging panel sits.

## Options considered

### Multiple nominations

| Option | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| **A — One nomination per nominator / account (REJECTED)** | Simple ops; kills volume and the "multiply chances" incentive. |
| **B — Multiple nominations per nominator (CHOSEN)** | Each packet is independent. Same project cannot be entered twice. |

### Winner set and when categories apply

| Option | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| **A — Categories only at Stage 2 (REJECTED)** | Stage 1 would rank a mixed pool. Unfair on organisational scale; panel would be inventing buckets late. |
| **B — Intake categories; one overall winner + three places per category (CHOSEN)** | Stage 1 already ranks within Small / Medium / Large. Stage 2 confirms three places per category and names the overall winner from the Stage 2 field. |

## Rules in force

### Multiple nominations

* A logged-in nominator can **start another nomination** while drafts or submitted packets already exist.
* **One project, one nomination.** Do not resubmit the same contracted engagement under a second reference.
* Each nomination needs its **own** client evaluation (unique link, same 31 Oct close).
* Headcount category is an **organisation** attribute. Several nominations from the same firm will usually sit in the **same** category and compete with each other.
* No published cap per nominator. The public constraint remains the **1 Sep – 31 Oct 2026** window (15 Jul: no "first 100" cap).

### Categories and winners

* **Assign at intake** (WS4 Stage C): Small 1–25, Medium 26–100, Large 101+, customer-project headcount only. Not chosen by the nominator. Not deferred to Stage 2.
* **Stage 1:** score independently, then **rank and shortlist per category**.
* **Stage 2:** presentations and rescoring. Panel names:
    * three places in **Small**, three in **Medium**, three in **Large**;
    * **one overall** Project Contractor of the Year, from the Stage 2 field (normally also a category first place).
* Empty places stay empty if the field is thin. Do not invent winners to fill a grid.
* Publication: overall winner, the three first places, and the remaining category places as case-profile capacity allows (consent required).

## Consequences

* **WIX:** Nominee dashboard keeps **Start new nomination** after the first packet. Do not switch the only CTA to "View nomination status".
* **WS4:** Record overall + 3×3 places at the winner gate. Shortlist size (N per category into Stage 2) stays an operations setting; it is not the same as the three named places.
* **WS3 / W1 workshop:** Say you can enter more than once. Say the titles: one overall, three places per category. Say intake assigns the category.
* **WS6:** Certificates and announcements carry category place **and** overall where both apply.
* **Guides / Overview / 1.0–3.0:** align to this record.
