## Decision

Two rules for the live P-CotY 2026 cycle (and until a later DR supersedes them):

1. **One nominator may submit multiple nominations.** Same account, more than one packet. Each nomination is a **distinct project**, with its own narrative, evidence, and client evaluation. This is allowed so one person can put more than one piece of work on the table and multiply their chances.
2. **Winner set.** Judges name **four awards**: one **overall** Project Contractor of the Year, plus **one award in each headcount category** (Small, Medium, Large). Categories are **assigned at intake** from customer-project headcount as a **label for the award title**, not as a separate scoring track. **Stage 1 and Stage 2 score every nomination the same way** — nine criteria, one shared field. Stage 2 **names** the four awards. It does **not** assign categories. Dual title is allowed (category winner may also be Overall). Thin categories may leave an award empty; do not invent winners.

## Context

The public CTA and some UAT-era copy treated a nominator as having a single nomination. That blocks a strong contractor (or a nominator putting several contractors forward) from entering more than one project.

Winner language was still soft: category winners, an overall title "if applicable", and "top-3 finalists" without a locked set. Staging of categories was already intake in WS4 / Overview; this record **rejects** any model that leaves category assignment until the judging panel sits.

**Amendment 2026-09-09:** The first accepted winner set (overall + three places per category; Stage 1 ranking inside category) is **superseded**. Judging is the same for every project. The panel names four awards only. Workshop W1, Overview, and Guides align to this amendment.

## Options considered

### Multiple nominations

| Option | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| **A — One nomination per nominator / account (REJECTED)** | Simple ops; kills volume and the "multiply chances" incentive. |
| **B — Multiple nominations per nominator (CHOSEN)** | Each packet is independent. Same project cannot be entered twice. |

### Winner set and when categories apply

| Option | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| **A — Categories only at Stage 2 (REJECTED)** | Panel would be inventing buckets late. |
| **B — Intake categories; overall + three places per category; Stage 1 ranks inside category (SUPERSEDED 2026-09-09)** | Nine named places in theory; separate ranking tracks by organisational scale. |
| **C — Intake categories as award labels only; shared-merit Stage 1 and Stage 2; four awards (CHOSEN)** | Same nine criteria for every nomination. Panel names Overall + Small + Medium + Large. Dual title OK. Fair headcount label without a separate race. |

## Rules in force

### Multiple nominations

- A logged-in nominator can **start another nomination** while drafts or submitted packets already exist.
- **One project, one nomination.** Do not resubmit the same contracted engagement under a second reference.
- Each nomination needs its **own** client evaluation (unique link, same 31 Oct close).
- Headcount category is an **organisation** attribute. Several nominations from the same firm will usually carry the **same** category label.
- No published cap per nominator. The public constraint remains the **1 Sep – 31 Oct 2026** window (15 Jul: no "first 100" cap).

### Categories and winners

- **Assign at intake** (WS4 Stage C): Small 1–25, Medium 26–100, Large 101+, customer-project headcount only. Not chosen by the nominator. Not deferred to Stage 2. The label is for the **category award title**, not for a separate scoring track.
- **Stage 1:** score independently on the nine criteria (1–10). **One shared field** — do not rank or shortlist inside category. Shortlist top N from the shared field (N is an operations setting).
- **Stage 2:** presentations and rescoring on the same nine criteria for every shortlisted project. Panel names **four awards**:
  - **one overall** Project Contractor of the Year;
  - **one** Small, **one** Medium, **one** Large category award.
- Dual title is allowed (Overall may also hold a category award). Empty category awards stay empty if the field is thin. Do not invent winners to fill a grid.
- Publication: the four award winners as case-profile capacity allows (consent required).

## Consequences

- **WIX:** Nominee dashboard keeps **Start new nomination** after the first packet. Do not switch the only CTA to "View nomination status".
- **WS4:** Record four awards at the winner gate (Overall + S/M/L). Shortlist size N from the **shared** Stage 1 field stays an operations setting.
- **WS3 / W1 workshop:** Say you can enter more than once. Say the titles: four awards — Overall, Small, Medium, Large. Say judging is the same for every project. Say intake assigns the category label.
- **WS6:** Certificates and announcements carry category award **and** overall where both apply.
- **Guides / Overview / 1.0–3.0:** align to this record (amended 2026-09-09).
