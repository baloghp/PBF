## Overview

The Nominee Coach guides assigned nominees through the full lifecycle: submission, Stage 1, Stage 2, and award.

## Key Responsibilities

1. Submission support and completeness checks
2. Stage 1 coordination (assessors, COI, moderation, shortlisting)
3. Stage 2 preparation and scheduling
4. Award notifications and publication coordination

## Core Checklists Included

The Confluence source includes detailed checklists for:

* Submission phase
* Stage 1 assessment
* Stage 2 preparation
* Award phase

It also provides escalation guidance, issue handling playbooks, template communications, and links to key process resources.
