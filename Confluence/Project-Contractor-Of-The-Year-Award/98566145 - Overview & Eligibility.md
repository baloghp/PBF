# Overview & Eligibility

This page summarizes the program design and who can apply.

## Eligibility

* Project type: cross‑corporate customer–contractor projects (across all industries) where the nominee acts/acted as an independent contractor for a paying client/customer under contract.
* Completion window: project completed within the last 2 years (2024-2025) or running at the moment of nomination.
* Evidence set: see [Submission Package](https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/98598913)
* Acceptance of publication of a short case profile.

## CotY specifics

* Two‑actor reality: both contractor and client perspectives are required. Each nomination must include a client‑completed assessment sheet, and we will operate a simple system to collect, validate, and store these forms (Confluence template + PDF upload; values captured in the nomination form).
* Two phases:

    * Stage 1 (written + client assessment) performed by trained volunteers;
    * Stage 2 (finalists) presented to a high‑profile panel of judges with Q&A and re‑scoring

## Categories

Categories are based on **headcount in customer projects** (not including internal projects, administration, or bookkeeping). This reflects the organization's scale and capacity for customer-facing project delivery.

* **Small organizations (1-25 people in customer projects):**

Includes freelancers and solo practitioners. Typically founder-driven, high individual accountability, flexible and responsive. May include freelancers working independently or small teams.

Project relevance: Projects often exploratory, defining products, markets, and identity. Strong individual accountability, adaptable to changing requirements.

* **Medium organizations (26-100 people in customer projects)**

Traits: Expanding customer base and workforce. Beginning formalization: processes, roles, and controls emerge. Strong drive for scaling and market capture. Often experience the "growing pains" of structure vs. agility.

Project relevance: Projects aim to build systems, infrastructure, and brand presence. Often under time pressure; governance still catching up.

* **Large organizations (101+ people in customer projects)**

Traits: Established customer base, institutionalized knowledge, predictable operations. More structured processes and governance. May have multiple concurrent customer projects. Stronger organizational capacity but may face challenges with agility and innovation.

Project relevance: Projects typically focus on large-scale delivery, system integration, and enterprise-level solutions. More formal governance structures in place.

**Note:** Category boundaries may be adjusted based on actual submission patterns. The goal is to ensure fair comparison within similar organizational scales.

## Evaluation Process (two stages)

* Stage 1 – Volunteer Review (screening)

    * Screen for eligibility and completeness (client sheet present, evidence set, COI declarations).
    * Apply the Evaluation Framework (0–5 scale) by at least two reviewers; shortlist top N per category.
    * **Notify all applicants of their shortlist status (shortlisted or not shortlisted).**
    * **Provide feedback to non-shortlisted applicants from the Project Business Foundation identifying areas that can be strengthened for future submissions.**
    
* Stage 2 – Judging Panel Presentation & Q&A (final)

    * Shortlisted nominees deliver a 20‑minute online presentation + 15‑minute Q&A to the selected jury.
    
        * The presentation will be recorded and can be published by PBF (data protection and authorization need to be collected. )
        
    * Jury composition: high‑profile experts (e.g., PMI community leaders) with relevant domain mix; COI/recusal enforced.
    * Jurors rescore using the same framework; client sheet and Q&A responses inform final deliberation.
    * Consensus or moderated average determines winners. (In case of stalemate or indecision <custom data-type="mention" data-id="id-0">@Oliver F. Lehmann</custom> has the final deciding vote)
