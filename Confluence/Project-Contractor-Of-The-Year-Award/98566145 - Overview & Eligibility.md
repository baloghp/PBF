# Overview & Eligibility

This page summarizes the program design and who can apply.

**Related:** [Submission Package](https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/98598913) · [Process Map](https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/117866524) · [Roles and RACI](https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/117964816)

---

## Eligibility

* Project type: cross‑corporate customer–contractor projects (across all industries) where the nominee acts/acted as an independent contractor for a paying client/customer under contract.
* Completion window: project completed within the last 2 years (2024–2025) or running at the moment of nomination.
* Evidence set: see [Submission Package](https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/98598913)
* Acceptance of publication of a short case profile.

---

## CotY specifics

* **Two‑actor reality:** both contractor and client perspectives are required. Each nomination must include a client‑completed assessment sheet, collected and validated via the nomination platform (see [Client Assessment Form](https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/98664449)).
* **Intake:** automated pre-screening checks eligibility and completeness within ~24 hours of submission; **Project Manager / Award Operations** handles exceptions. Missing items are flagged on your dashboard; **Communications Coordinator** sends templated notifications (see DR-002).
* **Nominee support:** track status on the platform dashboard; questions via **program support** / PMO inbox — **no Nominee Coach** volunteer for this cycle.
* **Two evaluation phases:**
    * **Stage 1** — independent **assessor** screening and scoring (Evaluation Framework, ≥2 assessors per nomination);
    * **Stage 2** — shortlisted finalists present to the **judging panel** (20‑minute presentation + 15‑minute Q&A, re‑scoring).

---

## Categories

Categories are based on **headcount in customer projects** (not including internal projects, administration, or bookkeeping). Categories are **assigned during intake** (not at submission). Shortlisting is per category.

* **Small organizations (1–25 people in customer projects):** freelancers, solo practitioners, small teams.
* **Medium organizations (26–100 people in customer projects):** scaling organizations with emerging formalization.
* **Large organizations (101+ people in customer projects):** established operations with structured governance.

**Note:** Category boundaries may be adjusted based on actual submission patterns. The goal is fair comparison within similar organizational scales.

---

## Evaluation Process (two stages)

### Intake (before Stage 1)

* Automated **pre-screening agent** validates eligibility, completeness, and required attachments (does **not** score nominations).
* **Communications Coordinator** sends missing-item notifications if anything is incomplete.
* **Project Manager / Award Operations** assigns **≥2 independent assessors** per complete, eligible nomination.

### Stage 1 – Independent assessor screening

* Apply the [Evaluation Framework](https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/98631681) (1–10 scale); **minimum ≥2 assessors** per nomination (target 3 when capacity allows).
* PM moderates inter-rater discrepancies; rank and shortlist top N per category.
* **Communications Coordinator** notifies all applicants (shortlisted / not shortlisted) and sends feedback to non-shortlisted nominees.

### Stage 2 – Judging Panel Presentation & Q&A

* Shortlisted nominees deliver a 20‑minute online presentation + 15‑minute Q&A to the selected jury.
* Presentations may be recorded and published by PBF (subject to consent).
* Jury composition: high‑profile experts with relevant domain mix; COI/recusal enforced.
* Jurors rescore using the same framework; client sheet and Q&A inform deliberation.
* Consensus or moderated average determines winners. (Stalemate: @Oliver F. Lehmann has the deciding vote.)
