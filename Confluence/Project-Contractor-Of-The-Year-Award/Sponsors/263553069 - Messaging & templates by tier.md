## 1. Messaging strategy (by tier)

| Tier | Who | Core pitch | Lead with | Ask | Don't say |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1 Amplification** *(primary)* | Tools, contractors, media, bodies | "Reach project leaders during **nomination season** — mutual LinkedIn lift" | Sep–Oct window; Showcase audience; **PCOTY supplies post creative** | **~1 post per comms phase** (anticipation / launch / FOMO) + reciprocal | "Sponsor us" / cash / product endorsement |
| **2 Community** | Alumni, small firms, volunteers' employers | "Low-friction way to back the community" | One repost or internal nudge | Single touch in Sep–Oct | Logo block / heavy commitment |
| **3 Services** | Design, legal, software, print | "Pro bono scoped help — credited where visible" | Specific deliverable + deadline | SOW with named output | Open-ended consulting |

### Deferred cycle 1 *(archive — do not lead outreach)*

| Tier | Note |
| --- | --- |
| **Founding** | Ceremony/travel/venue — not cycle 1 |
| **External prize** | Product/service to winners — not cycle 1 |


**Shared messages (all tiers)**

- Recognises **contractor–client project excellence** (Project Business), not generic “PM of the year”.
- **Nominations 1 Sep – 31 Oct 2026** → winners **Feb 2027**.
- Partners **never** influence who wins; support is **not required** to nominate or win.
- Wording: **Supported by** / **Amplification partner** — not product endorsement.
- Documentation (confirmation, email, SOW) → [Partnership legalities & confirmation](263520298 - Partnership legalities & confirmation.md).

## 2. Outreach email templates

### 2.1 First touch (all tiers — warm)

**Subject:** `Project Contractor of the Year — amplification partner (cycle 1)`

Hi [Name],

I'm [Peter / Oliver] from the team behind the **Project Contractor of the Year Award** — recognising excellence in **contractor–client project delivery**, presented by the **Project Business Foundation** and led by **Oliver F. Lehmann**.

We completed a **16-volunteer UAT** in June. **Public nominations open 1 September 2026** at [ittd.space/pbf-pcoty](https://www.ittd.space/pbf-pcoty). **Winner announcement Feb 2027 — virtual ceremony.**

We're a **volunteer programme** — we don't take cash. We're inviting aligned organisations as **amplification partners** (LinkedIn mutual lift during nomination season).

Would you be open to a **20-minute call** in [week]? I can send a one-page summary and ready-made post creative.

Best regards,  
[Name]
