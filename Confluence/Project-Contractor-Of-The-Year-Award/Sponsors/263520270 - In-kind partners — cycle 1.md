## 1. Model

Volunteer-run programme — **no cash sponsorship** in cycle 1. Partners contribute **goods, services, or direct payment to vendors** (not to the core team) for **defined recognition**.

| In-kind | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Amplification | LinkedIn posts, newsletter, internal nomination drive |
| Prizes *(cycle 1: internal only)* | PBF-led winner benefit (e.g. certification seats) — **not external prize partners** |
| Ceremony | **Virtual only cycle 1** — no venue/travel sponsorship tier |
| Operations | Design, printing, software, pro bono legal |

**Not in scope:** cash grants or reimbursements to individuals · sponsors influencing nominations/assessment/winners · product endorsement (use **Supported by** / **Prize courtesy of** only).

**Cycle context (DR-001):** nominations **1 Sep – 31 Oct 2026** · finalists **30 Nov** · winners **Feb 2027** — **fully virtual ceremony (cycle 1)**.

**15 Jul decision:** Drop **Founding** and **external Prize** tiers for cycle 1. Focus outreach on **Amplification** (+ Community / Services as needed). Oliver may pursue **press/media** (e.g. PM Welt) opportunistically — not a formal tier.

## 4. Governance & agreement

Governance rules (non-negotiable) and **partnership confirmation** templates → **[Partnership legalities & confirmation](263520298 - Partnership legalities & confirmation.md)**.

**Non-negotiable** (adapted from [Business Care Award rules](https://businesscareaward.com/en/award-rules.html)):

- Partners **must not** influence intake, assessor assignment, scoring, shortlist, or winners.
- Partner support **not required** to nominate, assess, or win.
- No partner on **judging panel** with voting rights.
- **Partnership confirmation + Schedule A** (Tiers 1–3) before public use of name/logo; PCOTY may decline COI or brand conflicts (WS5 / Programme Owner).
