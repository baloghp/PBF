# Submission Package

**Status:** Draft - Subject to change

This page outlines the complete submission package required for the PBF Contractor of the Year Award nomination.

---

## Overview

When you submit your nomination, you will be assigned a **Nominee Coach** who will guide you through the entire process from submission to award. Your coach is your single point of contact for all questions and support.

**Platform:** Nominations are submitted via SurveyMonkey (forms can be embedded in ITTD and PBF websites).

---

## Data Protection & Privacy (GDPR)

### Data Retention Policy

* **Retention Period:** All nomination materials will be retained for **1 year** after the award cycle completion
* **After 1 year:** Materials will be archived or deleted in accordance with our data retention policy
* **GDPR applies only to personal data** - Company/project data is not subject to GDPR requirements

### Minimize Personal Data in Submissions

**Important:** To comply with GDPR and protect privacy, **please minimize personal data** in your submission materials.

**What is Personal Data?**

* Names of individuals (unless essential for the narrative)
* Email addresses (use company emails only if necessary)
* Phone numbers
* Home addresses
* Personal identifiers
* Any information that can identify a specific individual

**Best Practices:**

* Use job titles/roles instead of names where possible (e.g., "Project Manager" instead of "John Smith")
* Use company/organization names instead of individual names
* Focus on project outcomes, processes, and organizational achievements
* If names are essential (e.g., for testimonials), obtain consent from those individuals
* Use generic descriptions: "the client's technical lead" rather than specific names

**Why This Matters:**

* Reduces GDPR compliance burden for both you and PBF
* Protects privacy of individuals mentioned in submissions
* Simplifies data retention and deletion processes
* Makes case studies easier to publish (less anonymization needed)

**If Personal Data is Necessary:**

* Only include what is essential for the nomination
* Ensure you have consent from individuals whose data you're sharing
* Be aware that this data will be retained for 1 year per our retention policy

## Required Components

### 1. Written Submission (≤ 2,000 words)

Complete a narrative covering:

* Objectives
* Scope/baseline
* Outcomes vs baseline
* Interface & governance
* Commercial/contract model and liquidity
* Risk handling
* Integrity controls

**Note:** You can save your submission as a draft and edit it before final submission.

**GDPR Reminder:** Minimize personal data - use roles/titles instead of names where possible.

---

### 2. What Makes This Project Exemplary? (500 words - Required)

**This is a key differentiator for winners.** Explain what sets this project apart from a "good" project and makes it truly exemplary.

Address the following:

* **What sets this apart?** - What makes this project exceptional, not just competent?
* **Innovation & Uniqueness** - What did you do differently? Novel approaches, pioneering techniques, or challenges to industry norms?
* **Broader Impact** - Beyond project success, what changed? How did this transform the client's business, industry, or community?
* **Industry Contribution** - How does this advance the field? What can others learn from this project?

**Evaluation:** This section is evaluated as part of the "Innovation & Industry Advancement" criterion. Winners should demonstrate they did something uniquely valuable that others can learn from.

---

### 3. Impact Measurement & Transformation (300 words - Required)

Provide evidence of measurable, lasting impact beyond project completion.

Include:

* **Quantifiable Outcomes** - Specific metrics demonstrating impact (e.g., "reduced processing time by 40%", "saved client £X million", "improved customer satisfaction by X%")
* **Transformation Evidence** - How did the project change the client's business, processes, or capabilities?
* **Long-term Benefits** - What impact remains 6-12 months after completion? What lasting value was created?
* **ROI or Value Demonstration** - Beyond contract value, what additional value was created for the client?

**Evaluation:** This section is evaluated as part of the "Project success (outcomes)" criterion, with emphasis on measurable, lasting impact.

---

### 4. Lessons Learned & Industry Contribution (300 words - Required)

Share knowledge that benefits the profession and demonstrates thought leadership.

Address:

* **What did you learn?** - Key insights that others should know
* **Knowledge Sharing** - How are you sharing this knowledge? (Publications, presentations, templates, mentoring, case studies)
* **Industry Contribution** - How has this project influenced your approach to future work? How might it influence others?
* **Replicability** - What practices or approaches from this project could others adopt?

**Evaluation:** This section is evaluated as part of the "Innovation & Industry Advancement" criterion, demonstrating contribution to the profession.

---

### 5. Evidence Requirements (Practical Artifacts)

Upload the following documents:

* **Draft contract matrix** - Mapping obligations, applicable law, jurisdiction, warranties/liability, notice and change mechanisms
* **Customer–contractor interface RACI** - Including provisions and enabling services, for inclusion in charter/contract attachments
* **People Development metric** - Percentage of net income/profit invested in staff development (technical, organizational, or commercial). Categories: 0%, 0-5%, or 5%+. This metric demonstrates investment in team capabilities that benefit both customer and contractor
* **Code of conduct acknowledgment** - Aligned to cross-corporate template
* **Integrity plan** - Aligned to cross-corporate template

**GDPR Reminder:** Ensure uploaded documents minimize personal data. Use roles/titles instead of names where possible.

---

### 6. Client Assessment Form

**Process:**

1. **Provide to Client:** You will provide a QR code or link to your client representative
2. **Client Completes Form:** The client representative will:

    * Rate 7 criteria (0-10 scale) with weights
    * Calculate Weight × Rating for each criterion
    * Provide total score and % achieved
    * Add evidence notes for ratings ≥8 or ≤4
    * Sign and provide consent for:
    
        * Evaluation and communications
        * Short story publication (if winner or top 3 finalist)
        
    
3. **Client Uploads:** Client uploads signed PDF (or Excel version)
4. **You Enter Score:** Enter the total score and % achieved in your nomination form

**Templates Available:**

* Template on the [Client Assessment Form page](https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/98664449)
* Excel version (when available)

---

### 7. Publication Consent

* Accept publication consent for short case profile
* This allows PBF to publish a short case story if you are selected as a winner or top 3 finalist
* Winners may be asked to participate in case study development

## After Submission

1. **Confirmation:** You will receive automatic confirmation with:

    * Submission reference number
    * Expected timeline for review
    * Link to track application status
    
2. **Nominee Coach Assignment:** A Nominee Coach will be assigned to guide you through the process
3. **Completeness Check:** Your coach will review your submission for completeness (within 24 hours)
4. **Status Updates:** You can track your application status: Submitted → Under Review → Eligible/Ineligible → Shortlisted/Not Shortlisted → Finalist/Not Finalist → Winner/Not Winner
