# Assessor Guide (Stage 1)

**Parent page:** [Roles and RACI](https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/117964816)

This guide defines Stage 1 assessor responsibilities: COI checks, eligibility/completeness verification, independent scoring on the 0–5 framework, evidence-based comments, submission deadlines, and optional support for Stage 2 finalist preparation.

The source page contains:

* Criterion-by-criterion scoring guidance (including Innovation & Industry Advancement as the key differentiator)
* Assessment checklists (pre, during, post)
* Common issue handling (COI, incomplete submissions, scoring disputes)
* Escalation paths and resource links.
