# Assessor Guide (Stage 1)

**Parent:** [Assessor Recruitment Workstream](284164097 - Assessor Recruitment Workstream — Cycle 1.md) · was under [Roles and RACI](117964816 - Roles and RACI.md)

**This page = how to score.** Portal/form behaviour: [Platform Spec](284196865 - Stage 1 Assessor Platform Spec.md). Framework detail: [Evaluation Framework](98631681 - Evaluation Framework.md).

---

## Your job

Independently score assigned nominations on **9 criteria (0–5)** with justification comments. Finish by the deadline.

**You do:** COI confirm · read the packet · score · comment · submit.  
**You do not:** eligibility/completeness checks (Admins gate that) · coach nominees · share scores with nominees.

**COI:** If you have a conflict, stop and contact **organisers** (registration channel) for reassignment. Do not score that nomination.

There is **no Nominee Coach**. Questions → organisers / Admin.

---

## Scale

| Score | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Not demonstrated / missing evidence |
| 1 | Poor |
| 2 | Fair |
| 3 | Good |
| 4 | Very good |
| 5 | Excellent |

---

## Criteria (score each; comment required)

| # | Criterion | Look for | Ask |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Project Success (Outcomes) | Delivery vs baseline; benefits; handover; measurable impact / transformation | Did they deliver? What lasting impact? |
| 2 | Agility & Adaptability | Change control; fit to contracting model (incl. T&M cash/visibility) | How did they handle change without chaos? |
| 3 | Commercial Model (Liquidity) | Price realism; milestones; advances/retentions; liquidity both sides | Fair cash flow for both parties? |
| 4 | Legal Soundness | Law/jurisdiction; warranty/liability; privity; fit to collaboration model | Complete, enforceable, suited to the model? |
| 5 | Interface and Governance | Customer–contractor boundary; escalation; decision rights | Clear boundary and working governance? |
| 6 | Risk Management (Cross-Corporate) | Cross-party financial, ops, IP, cyber, regulatory, reputational, geopolitical risks + mitigations | Risks identified and mitigated across the interface? |
| 7 | People Development | Investment as % of profit: 0 / 0–5 / 5%+; training/certs evidence | Real investment with delivery impact? |
| 8 | Team & Business Acumen | Leadership; customer vs profit balance; code of conduct; anti-corruption; transparency | Integrity and leadership under pressure? |
| 9 | **Innovation & Industry Advancement** | Novelty; industry contribution; knowledge sharing; case-study worth | Would we publish this? Winners typically **4–5** here. |

---

## Comment tips

Per criterion: why this score · strengths · gaps · evidence pointers (section/artifact). Be objective, consistent, constructive.

**Credo check for winners:** excellence · impact · industry contribution · innovation · legacy — not merely competent delivery.

**Red flags:** missing evidence · no measurable impact · generic approach · no knowledge sharing · integrity concerns · 0% people development.  
**Green flags:** clear transformation · real innovation · active sharing · strong cross-party risk & integrity · 5%+ people development · case-study worthy.

---

## After submit

You may be asked to join **moderation** if scores diverge. Discuss evidence, not ego. Do not share scores with nominees — refer them to organisers.
