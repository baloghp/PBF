## Client Assessment Sheet (required)

* Because Contractor of the Year spans two actors (customer and contractor), each nomination must include a simple, signed client assessment sheet completed by the customer representative.
* Format mirrors Oliver's monthly award fee sheet concept: weighted criteria with 0–10 ratings and W×R scoring, plus total and % achieved.
* Mandatory fields: customer org, project, period covered, signatory name/role/date, contact email; consent for publication of aggregate score, narrative, and short story (if selected as winner or top 3 finalist).
* Mapped to our criteria above as follows (suggested starting weights; can be tuned per category):

    * Communication responsiveness (W=10)
    * Human resources aptitude & teamwork (W=7)
    * Safety & compliance communication (W=9)
    * Documentation timeliness & quality (W=6)
    * Change responsiveness & management (W=5)
    * Management attention (W=8)
    * Execution pace/correctness/attentiveness (W=10)
    
* Upload the completed sheet as PDF plus enter the totals in the nomination form.
