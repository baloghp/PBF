# THROUGH EMAIL

**SUBJECT: Call for Volunteer Assessors for Project Contractor of the Year Award**

Dear Fellow Project Professionals,

We are pleased to invite experienced **PMPs/PBPs** to contribute as **Volunteer Assessors** for the **Project Contractor of the Year Award**.

The Award has been established to recognize contractors that demonstrate excellence not only in delivering projects, but also in managing the wider **Project Business**, creating value for clients and stakeholders while effectively managing delivery, commercial, contractual, and project-related challenges.

### Become a Volunteer Assessor

The assessment process is designed to be **structured, consistent, and evidence-based**, with all participating contractors assessed against the same established criteria.

As a Volunteer Assessor, you will have the opportunity to apply your professional experience to the evaluation of candidatures.

Assessors will be asked to:

Review the candidatures submitted by participating contractors.

- Evaluate the evidence and information provided by each contractor.
- Assess project performance against the **established Award criteria**.
- Score each candidature using the defined assessment methodology.
- Apply professional judgement based on your project management and project business experience.

### Why Participate?

Volunteering as an assessor is an opportunity to:

- Contribute to recognizing and promoting **excellence in project contracting**.
- Gain insight into how leading contractors approach project delivery and project business management.
- Apply and further develop your professional assessment and analytical skills.
- Contribute to establishing benchmarks and sharing good practices across the project community.
- Support an initiative dedicated to recognizing excellence on the **contractor side of project business**.

Further information about the Award, assessment framework, and initiative is available here:

**Awards Website:** [https://www.ittd.space/pbf-pcoty](https://www.ittd.space/pbf-pcoty)

**LinkedIn Page:** [https://www.linkedin.com/showcase/129664440/](https://www.linkedin.com/showcase/129664440/)

If you are an experienced project professional and would like to contribute as a Volunteer Assessor, please reply to this email or express your interest through our website and/or LinkedIn page.

We would be delighted to welcome experienced members of the **PMP/PBP community** to the assessor network and collectively recognize the contractors that are setting new standards for excellence in project delivery.

**Kind Regards,**

The assessment focuses on established criteria covering areas such as project delivery, project management, commercial performance, stakeholder management, risk management, and business value creation.

As a Volunteer Assessor, you would be asked to:

- Review the candidatures submitted by participating contractors.
- Evaluate the information and evidence provided by each candidate.

- Score each candidature objectively and independently using the provided assessment framework.
- Apply your professional judgement and project experience when evaluating the submitted evidence.

Your involvement would not only support the credibility and professional integrity of the Award but would also provide an opportunity to contribute to the recognition of **best practices and excellence in Project Contracting and Project Business Management**.
