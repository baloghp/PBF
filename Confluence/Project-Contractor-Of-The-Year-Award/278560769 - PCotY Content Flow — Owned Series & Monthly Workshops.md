> **Draft for team review — on the 28 Jul agenda.** A proposal to shape the LinkedIn backlog into an editorial system: each member **owns a series**, series **cross-reference** each other, and **each month builds to an Oliver-hosted workshop**.

**The idea.** Each member **owns one topic and runs it as a series**. Series cross-reference each other so the page reads as one conversation. **Each month builds to a 1-hour Oliver workshop** that synthesizes the theme; the workshop is recorded → clipped → those clips become next month's posts. That's the flywheel.

**How it publishes.** Owners draft on their **own LinkedIn profile**; the **PCOTY page reshares the best** and teammates comment within the first hour. This compounds personal reach while keeping the page curated. No duplicate same-day posting.

## 2. Monthly arc

| Month | Theme | 🎤 Oliver workshop | CTA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Aug — Anticipation** | *What the award stands for* | **W1 — 31 Aug / 1 Sep: "Meet PCotY"** (2 sessions for time zones) | Follow · save the date |
| **Sep — Launch** | *How to participate & win* | **W2 — mid-Sep: "How to nominate & what judges look for"** | Nominate · apply as assessor |
| **Oct — FOMO / close** | *The stakes & the craft* | **W3 — early Oct: "The craft of great contracting"** | Submit before 31 Oct |

**Workshop rhythm:** promote 2 weeks ahead (owner posts point to it) → run it → post recap + 2–3 clips the following week. Each workshop is the culmination of the month's series, and its clips fuel the next month.

## 3. Weekly cadence (weekdays only)

| Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundational — 🎯/🌍/🤖 | ⏳ Countdown — 🎨 | Craft / how-to — 🏆/🎨/🤖 | ⏳ Countdown — 🎨 | Provocation / lens — 🔥/🧭/🎨 |

**Cross-reference rule:** every post ends with a one-line pointer to a sibling series (tags the teammate → internal links + algorithm boost), and all series reuse the same 3–4 phrases (*Project Business*, *the seller side*, *fair risk-sharing*) so the arc feels intentional.
