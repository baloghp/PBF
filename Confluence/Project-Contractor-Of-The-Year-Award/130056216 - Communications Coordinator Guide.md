## Overview

The Communications Coordinator owns centralized communications, templates, publication planning, embargo handling, and public award announcements.

## Main responsibilities from the source page

* Build communication strategy and template library
* Support Nominee Coaches with consistent messaging
* Produce award letters/certificates and publication materials
* Coordinate embargo and publication consent
* Publish results on ITTD and PBF channels
* Run post-award feedback and archive activities

The original page includes detailed templates and operational checklists for each phase.
