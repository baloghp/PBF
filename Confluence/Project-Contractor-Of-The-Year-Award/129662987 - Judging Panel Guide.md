# Judging Panel Guide (Stage 2)

**Parent page:** [Roles and RACI](https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/117964816)

This guide covers Stage 2 judging expectations:

* Attend finalist presentations and Q&A
* Score all 9 criteria independently (0–5)
* Focus strongly on Criterion 9: Innovation & Industry Advancement
* Participate in outlier moderation and final deliberation

The page also includes deliberation protocol, winner decision factors, scenario guidance, and practical checklists for before/during/after presentations.
