# 2.0 Assessment

This section covers the two-stage assessment process: Stage 1 (Volunteer Screening) and Stage 2 (Judging Panel Evaluation).

**Parent page:** [Process Map](https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/117866524)

---

## 2.1 Stage 1 – Volunteer Screening and Evaluation

**Objective:** Screen for eligibility/completeness, apply evaluation framework, and shortlist top candidates per category

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Assignment to Assessors] --> B[COI Check]
    B --> C{COI Identified?}
    C -->|Yes| D[Document Recusal]
    D --> E[Assign Alternate Assessor]
    E --> B
    C -->|No| F[Eligibility & Completeness Verification]
    F --> G[Apply Evaluation Framework]
    G --> H[Independent Scoring 0-5]
    I[Multiple Assessors] --> J[Inter-rater Reliability Check]
    J --> K{Discrepancy >2 points?}
    K -->|Yes| L[Moderation Discussion]
    L --> M[Calculate Moderated Average]
    K -->|No| M
    M --> N[Rank by Category]
    N --> O{Top N per Category?}
    O -->|Yes| P[Shortlisted - Advance to Stage 2]
    O -->|No| Q[Not Shortlisted - Provide Feedback]
```

Key activities include assessor assignment, COI recusal handling, independent scoring across all 9 criteria, inter-rater moderation, category ranking, and applicant notifications.

---

## 2.2 Stage 2 – Judging Panel Presentation & Q&A

**Objective:** Final evaluation of shortlisted nominees through presentations and Q&A, determine winners

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Finalist Preparation] --> B[Schedule Presentations]
    B --> C[Judging Panel Preparation]
    C --> D[COI Verification]
    D --> E[Conduct Presentation]
    E --> F[20-min Presentation + 15-min Q&A]
    F --> G[Panel Independent Scoring]
    G --> H{Outlier >2 points?}
    H -->|Yes| I[Outlier Moderation]
    I --> J[Consolidate Scores]
    H -->|No| J
    J --> K[Panel Deliberation]
    K --> L{Consensus?}
    L -->|No| M{Stalemate?}
    M -->|Yes| N[Oliver Lehmann Deciding Vote]
    M -->|No| O[Moderated Average]
    L -->|Yes| P[Winner Selected]
```

Finalists present and answer Q&A; judges rescore with emphasis on exemplariness and Innovation & Industry Advancement.
