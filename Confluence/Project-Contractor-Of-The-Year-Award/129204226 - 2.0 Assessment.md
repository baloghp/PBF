# 2.0 Assessment

This section covers the two-stage assessment process: Stage 1 (independent assessor screening) and Stage 2 (Judging Panel evaluation).

**Parent page:** [Process Map](https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/117866524)

**Prerequisite:** Nominations are **eligible and complete** after [1.0 Intake](https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/130514945) (automated pre-screening + PM exceptions). Stage 1 focuses on **scoring and shortlist**, not first-pass completeness.

---

## 2.1 Stage 1 – Independent Assessor Screening and Evaluation

**Objective:** Apply the evaluation framework, score independently, and produce a shortlist from **one shared field**. Categories were assigned at intake as award labels (DR-003); Stage 1 does not invent them and does not rank inside category.

**Accountability:** **Project Manager / Award Operations** — assessor assignment (≥2 per nomination), COI/quorum, moderation, shortlist lock. **Communications Coordinator** sends shortlist / not-shortlisted notifications (Stage E — not part of 2.1 scoring).

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[PM Assigns ≥2 Assessors] --> B[COI Check]
    B --> C{COI Identified?}
    C -->|Yes| D[Document Recusal]
    D --> E[PM Assigns Alternate]
    E --> B
    C -->|No| F[Optional Spot-check at Assessment]
    F --> G[Apply Evaluation Framework]
    G --> H[Independent Scoring 1-10]
    I[Multiple Assessors] --> J[Inter-rater Reliability Check]
    J --> K{Discrepancy >2 points?}
    K -->|Yes| L[PM Moderation]
    L --> M[Calculate Moderated Average]
    K -->|No| M
    M --> N[PM Ranks by Category]
    N --> O{Top N per Category?}
    O -->|Yes| P[Shortlisted - Advance to Stage 2]
    O -->|No| Q[Not Shortlisted]
```

**Key activities (Stage 1 ops — PM / WS4):** Assign ≥2 assessors per nomination; track COI recusals; moderate inter-rater discrepancies; shortlist top N from the shared field; lock shortlist.

**Key activities (Assessors):** Independent scoring on all 9 criteria; evidence-based comments; optional finalist prep support if requested by PM.

**RACI (2.1):**

* **Responsible:** Assessors (scoring)
* **Accountable:** Project Manager (assignment, COI/quorum, moderation, shortlist lock)
* **Consulted:** Assessors, Communications Coordinator (notification timing after shortlist)
* **Informed:** Applicant (via Comms after shortlist decision)

---

## 2.2 Stage 2 – Judging Panel Presentation & Q&A

**Objective:** Final evaluation of shortlisted nominees through presentations and Q&A. Name **four awards**: **Overall**, **Small**, **Medium**, and **Large**. Stage 2 does **not** assign categories. Scoring is the same for every shortlisted project.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Finalist Preparation] --> B[Schedule Presentations]
    B --> C[Judging Panel Preparation]
    C --> D[COI Verification]
    D --> E[Conduct Presentation]
    E --> F[20-min Presentation + 15-min Q&A]
    F --> G[Panel Independent Scoring]
    G --> H{Outlier >2 points?}
    H -->|Yes| I[Outlier Moderation]
    I --> J[Consolidate Scores]
    H -->|No| J
    J --> K[Panel Deliberation]
    K --> L{Consensus?}
    L -->|No| M{Stalemate?}
    M -->|Yes| N[Oliver Lehmann Deciding Vote]
    M -->|No| O[Moderated Average]
    L -->|Yes| P[Four awards named]
```

Finalists present and answer Q&A; judges rescore with emphasis on exemplariness and Innovation & Industry Advancement. Panel names four awards — Overall, Small, Medium, Large (DR-003 amended 2026-09-09). Dual title allowed.
