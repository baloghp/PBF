## External benchmark (PMI awards - Project of the year, and PMO of the year )

### Eligibility

* Projects complete within \~18 months of nomination deadline; any industry/region; nominator needn't be PMI-affiliated. Source: [pmi.org](http://pmi.org/) – Project of the Year
* PMO of the Year open to PMOs worldwide; focus on strategy alignment and value delivery. Source: [pmi.org](http://pmi.org/) – PMO Awards

### Categories (Project of the Year)

* Engineering/Technology,
* Social Impact,
* Construction/Infrastructure;
* category winners feed into overall Project of the Year. Source: [pmi.org](http://pmi.org/) – Project of the Year

### Process and deliverables

* Phase 1: Written submission (up to \~2,000 words) with narrative on goals, challenges, innovations, outcomes; release/consent forms. Finalists are shortlisted from written reviews. Source: [pmi.org](http://pmi.org/) – Project of the Year
* Phase 2: Finalist virtual presentation + ~~juror~~ judge/judging panel Q&A; judges synthesize scores for winners. Source: [pmi.org](http://pmi.org/) – Project of the Year
* PMO award uses a structured application (org profile, PMO scope, governance, value proof, innovation) and panel evaluation; finalists present virtually. Source: [pmi.org](http://pmi.org/) – PMO Awards Timeline (illustrative recent cycle)
* Project of the Year: nominations Feb–Apr; finalist presentations May; category notifications in Aug; winners at Global Summit in Nov. Source: [pmi.org](http://pmi.org/) – Project of the Year

### Judging model

Two-stage review (paper + presentation), independent judges, predefined criteria; overall winner selected from category winners (Project of the Year). Sources: Project of the Year, PMO

### Awards

Recognition Winners announced at PMI Global Summit; media amplification and case storytelling. Sources: Project of the Year, PMO Awards Sources: PMI Project of the Year; PMI PMO of the Year.
