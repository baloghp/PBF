# Judging Panel Guide (Stage 2)

**Parent page:** [Roles and RACI](https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/117964816)

---

## Overview

The Judging Panel conducts final evaluation of shortlisted nominees through presentations and Q&A sessions, and determines winners. Panel members are high-profile experts with relevant domain expertise.

---

## Key Responsibilities

### Stage 2 Evaluation (2.2)

* Attend presentations for shortlisted nominees
* Score finalists independently using Evaluation Framework (0-5 scale)
* Ask questions during Q&A using standardized question template
* Participate in outlier moderation discussions
* Deliberate on winners per category

---

## Quick Reference: Your Role in 3 Steps

1. **Attend presentations** (20 min + 15 min Q&A per finalist)
2. **Score independently** (0-5 scale, 9 criteria) - **Focus: Innovation & Industry Advancement (Criterion 9)**
3. **Deliberate and decide** winners per category

**Time commitment:** ~1 hour per finalist (presentation + scoring + deliberation)

---

3. **COI verification:**

   - You'll be notified of any recusals

## During Presentations: What to Do

### Presentation Format

- **20 minutes:** Finalist presents (max 10 slides)
- **15 minutes:** Q&A session
- **You:** Take notes, score independently

### What to Look For

**Required coverage areas:**
- Outcomes vs baseline
- Interface/governance
- Commercial/liquidity
- Risk management
- Integrity controls
- **What makes this exemplary?** ⭐
- **Measurable impact and transformation** ⭐
- **Lessons learned and industry contribution** ⭐

**Key question to answer:** *Is this truly exemplary, not just competent?*

### Q&A: Focus Areas

**Key areas to probe (use your professional judgment):**

- **Exemplariness:** What makes this truly exemplary, not just competent?
- **Innovation:** What did they do differently? Novel approaches?
- **Industry Contribution:** How does this advance the profession?
- **Impact:** Measurable, lasting impact beyond project completion?
- **Knowledge Sharing:** How are they sharing learnings with others?
- **Case Study Worthiness:** Would we want to publish this? Would others learn from this?

**Trust your expertise** to formulate questions that probe these areas effectively.

## Scoring: Quick Guide

### Scoring Scale (0-5)

- **0:** Not demonstrated / missing evidence
- **1:** Poor – Standard approach, no innovation
- **2:** Fair – Some novel elements, limited contribution
- **3:** Good – Solid innovation, basic knowledge sharing
- **4:** Very Good – Significant innovation, active knowledge sharing, replicable
- **5:** Excellent – Groundbreaking, advances profession, case study worthy

### The 9 Criteria (Score Each 0-5)

**1. Project Success (Outcomes)**
- Delivery vs baseline, measurable impact, transformation

**2. Agility & Adaptability**
- Responsiveness to change, effective change management

**3. Commercial Model (Liquidity Impact)**
- Price realism, cash flow, fair risk-sharing

**4. Legal Soundness**
- Complete, enforceable contract, appropriate terms

**5. Interface and Governance**
- Clear boundaries, effective governance, escalation paths

**6. Risk Management (Cross-Corporate)**
- Risk identification, mitigations, contingency planning

**7. People Development**
- Investment % (0%, 0-5%, 5%+), development activities, impact

**8. Team and Business Acumen (Integrity)**
- Leadership, integrity controls, transparency

**9. Innovation & Industry Advancement** ⭐ **CRITICAL**
- Innovation & uniqueness
- Industry contribution
- Broader impact
- **Case study worthiness** - Would we publish this?

**Winners should score 4-5 on Criterion 9.**

### Scoring Decision Tree

**Ask yourself for each criterion:**

1. **Is evidence present?** → If no: Score 0-1
2. **Is it standard practice?** → If yes: Score 1-2
3. **Is it good practice?** → If yes: Score 3
4. **Is it exemplary?** → If yes: Score 4-5
5. **Is it case study worthy?** → If yes: Score 5 (especially Criterion 9)

### What Makes a Winner?

**The Award Credo - Winners demonstrate:**

- ✅ **Beyond Competence → Excellence**
- ✅ **Beyond Compliance → Impact**
- ✅ **Beyond Project Success → Industry Contribution**
- ✅ **Beyond Standard Practice → Innovation**
- ✅ **Beyond Completion → Legacy**

**Deciding question:** *Would we want to publish this as a case study? Would others learn from this?*

## After Presentations: Scoring & Deliberation

### Step 1: Submit Your Scores

**Submit immediately after each presentation:**

1. **Score all 9 criteria** (0-5 each)
2. **Provide brief justification** for Criterion 9 (Innovation & Industry Advancement)
3. **Note any concerns** or questions
4. **Submit via platform** (link provided)

**Deadline:** Within 24 hours of presentation

### Step 2: Outlier Moderation (If Needed)

**If your score differs >2 points from median:**

1. **Facilitator will contact you**
2. **Brief discussion** (5-10 minutes)
3. **You may:**
   - Adjust score with justification
   - Maintain score with explanation
4. **Document decision**

**Goal:** Ensure fair evaluation, not force consensus

### Step 3: Deliberation Session

**After all presentations complete:**

1. **Review top candidates** per category
2. **Consider:**
   - Overall scores across all criteria
   - **Criterion 9 scores** (Innovation & Industry Advancement) - **Key differentiator**
   - Client Assessment scores
   - Presentation quality
   - Q&A responses
   - **Case study worthiness**

3. **Deliberate:**
   - Discuss top candidates
   - Reach consensus OR use moderated average
   - **If stalemate:** Oliver Lehmann has final deciding vote

4. **Select winners:**
   - One winner per category (Small, Medium, Large)
   - Overall Contractor of the Year (if applicable)

5. **Document rationale:**
   - Brief statement of why each winner was selected
   - Key strengths identified

**Time:** 1-2 hours for deliberation session

---

## Decision-Making Protocol

### Consensus vs Moderated Average

**Prefer consensus when:**
- Panel agrees on clear winner
- Scores are close
- Discussion clarifies differences

**Use moderated average when:**
- Scores vary but are reasonable
- Panel cannot reach consensus
- Time constraints require efficiency

### Stalemate Resolution

**If panel cannot decide:**
- Document the stalemate
- Oliver Lehmann (Programme Owner) makes final decision
- Document final decision rationale

### Winner Selection Criteria

**Priority order:**

1. **Criterion 9 (Innovation & Industry Advancement) score 4-5** - Must be exemplary
2. **Overall excellence** across all criteria
3. **Case study worthiness** - Would we publish this?
4. **Client Assessment scores** - Client perspective matters
5. **Presentation quality** - Ability to articulate value

**Remember:** We're looking for **exemplary**, not just **competent**.

## Common Scenarios & Quick Answers

### Scenario: "All finalists are good, how do I choose?"

**Answer:** Focus on Criterion 9 (Innovation & Industry Advancement). Which one:
- Did something uniquely valuable?
- Would make the best case study?
- Others would learn most from?
- Truly advances the profession?

### Scenario: "Scores are very close"

**Answer:** Use moderated average, but also consider:
- Which is more case study worthy?
- Which has stronger industry contribution?
- Which demonstrates more innovation?

### Scenario: "I disagree with other panel members"

**Answer:** This is normal. In moderation:
- Explain your rationale
- Listen to others' perspectives
- Focus on evidence, not opinions
- Use moderated average if needed

### Scenario: "Finalist didn't answer my question well"

**Answer:** Consider:
- Did they address it in written submission?
- Was it a communication issue or substance issue?
- Score based on evidence, not just presentation skills

### Scenario: "I identify a COI during presentation"

**Answer:**
1. Notify facilitator immediately
2. Recuse yourself from this finalist
3. Do not score or participate in deliberation for this finalist
4. Document recusal
