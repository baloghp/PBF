# RACI - Task-Based (KISS Approach)

**Principle:** Define roles based on actual tasks in the process steps, not management layers. Keep it simple.

---

## Core Roles (From Process Steps)

### Programme Owner
**Accountability:** Final decision authority

**Key Tasks:**
* Final approval of winners (3.1)
* Final deciding vote in stalemate (2.2)
* Strategic oversight

---

### Contact Person (PBF Side)
**Responsibility:** Single point of contact for all nominees

**Key Tasks:**
* Respond to applicant questions (1.2)
* Notify nominees of status changes (1.2, 2.1, 3.1)
* Coordinate with nominees throughout process
* Act as "gatekeeper" for all PBF-nominee communications

**Note:** This is Oliver's requirement - one person assigned as single point of contact for each nominee.

---

### Intake Coordinator
**Responsibility:** Handle intake and validation

**Key Tasks:**
* Check completeness of submissions (1.2)
* Verify eligibility criteria (1.2)
* Assign nominations to assessors (1.2)
* Update application status in platform (1.2)
* Handle incomplete applications (1.2)

---

### Assessment Coordinator
**Responsibility:** Coordinate assessment process

**Key Tasks:**
* Assign nominations to assessors (2.1)
* Document COI recusals (2.1)
* Assign alternate assessors when needed (2.1)
* Check inter-rater reliability (2.1)
* Moderate scoring discrepancies (2.1)
* Calculate scores and rank nominations (2.1)
* Prepare finalists for Stage 2 (2.2)
* Schedule presentations (2.2)
* Verify COI for judging panel (2.2)
* Facilitate Stage 2 presentations (2.2)
* Consolidate panel scores (2.2)

---

### Assessors (Volunteers - Stage 1)
**Responsibility:** Stage 1 evaluation

**Key Tasks:**
* Review assigned nominations (2.1)
* Check for COI and self-report (2.1)
* Verify eligibility and completeness (2.1)
* Score using Evaluation Framework (2.1)
* Provide comments and evidence notes (2.1)

---

### Judging Panel (Stage 2)
**Responsibility:** Final evaluation and winner selection

**Key Tasks:**
* Attend presentations (2.2)
* Score finalists independently (2.2)
* Ask questions during Q&A (2.2)
* Participate in outlier moderation (2.2)
* Deliberate on winners (2.2)

---

### Communications Coordinator
**Responsibility:** All communications and publications

**Key Tasks:**
* Prepare winner notifications (3.1)
* Prepare non-winner notifications (3.1)
* Send all notifications (3.1)
* Generate award letters/certificates (3.2)
* Prepare publication materials (3.2)
* Publish award results (3.2)
* Handle post-award activities (3.2)

---

## External Roles

### Applicant / Nominee
**Responsibility:** Submit nomination package

**Key Tasks:**
* Complete written submission (1.1)
* Upload evidence artifacts (1.1)
* Obtain Client Assessment Form (1.1)
* Submit nomination (1.1)
* Prepare Stage 2 presentation if shortlisted (2.2)
* **Designate a contact person** as single point of contact for PBF

---

### Client Representative
**Responsibility:** Complete Client Assessment Form

**Key Tasks:**
* Complete Client Assessment Form (1.1)
* Rate criteria and calculate scores (1.1)
* Sign and provide consent (1.1)

---

## RACI by Process Step

### 1.1 Nomination Submission
* **Responsible:** Applicant
* **Accountable:** Contact Person (PBF Side)
* **Consulted:** Intake Coordinator
* **Informed:** Programme Owner

### 1.2 Intake and Initial Validation
* **Responsible:** Intake Coordinator
* **Accountable:** Contact Person (PBF Side)
* **Consulted:** Assessment Coordinator
* **Informed:** Applicant, Programme Owner

### 2.1 Stage 1 – Volunteer Screening
* **Responsible:** Assessors
* **Accountable:** Assessment Coordinator
* **Consulted:** Intake Coordinator
* **Informed:** Applicant, Contact Person (PBF Side)

### 2.2 Stage 2 – Judging Panel
* **Responsible:** Judging Panel
* **Accountable:** Programme Owner
* **Consulted:** Assessment Coordinator (facilitation)
* **Informed:** Applicant, Contact Person (PBF Side)

### 3.1 Winner Selection and Notification
* **Responsible:** Communications Coordinator
* **Accountable:** Programme Owner
* **Consulted:** Assessment Coordinator, Judging Panel
* **Informed:** Applicant, Contact Person (PBF Side)

### 3.2 Award Publication
* **Responsible:** Communications Coordinator
* **Accountable:** Programme Owner
* **Consulted:** Assessment Coordinator, Judging Panel
* **Informed:** Applicant, Public

---

## Role Assignment (Small Team)

**Core Team (5 people):**
1. Programme Owner
2. Contact Person (PBF Side) - **Single point of contact for all nominees**
3. Intake Coordinator
4. Assessment Coordinator
5. Communications Coordinator

**Plus Volunteers:**
* 2-3 Assessors per nomination (Stage 1)
* 3-5 Judging Panel members (Stage 2)

---

## Key Simplifications

1. **Removed "Lead" roles** - Focus on task-based roles (Coordinator, not Lead)
2. **Contact Person (PBF Side)** - Explicitly defined as single point of contact (Oliver's requirement)
3. **Assessment Coordinator** - Handles both Stage 1 coordination and Stage 2 facilitation
4. **No separate Moderator/Facilitator** - Assessment Coordinator does this
5. **No separate Program Secretariat** - Assessment Coordinator handles COI documentation
6. **No separate Platform Administrator** - Intake Coordinator handles platform updates

---

## Contact Person Model

**For each nominee:**
* PBF assigns one **Contact Person (PBF Side)** at the start
* Nominee designates one **Contact Person (Nominee Side)** in their submission
* All communications flow through these two contact persons
* This ensures clear communication channels and accountability

---

_This RACI focuses on actual tasks from the process steps, with Contact Person as the key communication role per Oliver's guidance._
