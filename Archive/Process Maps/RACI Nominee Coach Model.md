# RACI - Nominee Coach Model (KISS Approach)

> **SUPERSEDED — ARCHIVED for audit only.** Replaced by post-UAT model in [DR-002 — Drop Nominee Coach role (Post-UAT)](https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/245989377) and [Roles and RACI](https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/117964816). **Do not use for the live P-CotY 2026 cycle.**

**Principle (historical):** One team member assigned to each nominee at nomination time. This person guides the nominee through the entire process from submission to award.

---

## Core Roles

### Project Manager
**Accountability:** Overall program success and operational coordination

**Key Tasks:**
* Final approval of winners (3.1)
* Final deciding vote in stalemate (2.2)
* Strategic oversight
* Assign Nominee Coaches to nominees (ensuring equal distribution across categories)
* **Create guidance documents for each role** (Nominee Coach, Assessors, Judging Panel, Communications Coordinator)
* **Ensure all roles are aware of their responsibilities** and have access to guidance documents
* **Act as central block remover** - identify and resolve obstacles that prevent roles from performing their tasks
* Coordinate between roles when needed
* Monitor overall process progress

---

### Nominee Coach
**Responsibility:** Guide assigned nominees through the entire process

**Key Tasks:**

**During Submission (1.1-1.2):**
* Act as single point of contact for assigned nominees
* Respond to nominee questions
* Check completeness of submissions
* Verify eligibility criteria
* Update application status in platform
* Handle incomplete applications and follow-ups

**During Stage 1 Assessment (2.1):**
* Assign assessors to their nominees' nominations
* Document COI recusals for their nominees
* Assign alternate assessors when needed
* Check inter-rater reliability for their nominees
* Moderate scoring discrepancies for their nominees
* Calculate scores and rank their nominees
* Notify their nominees of shortlist status

**During Stage 2 Preparation (2.2):**
* Prepare their shortlisted nominees for presentations
* Schedule presentations for their nominees
* Coordinate with assessors to help nominees prepare
* Verify COI for judging panel (for their nominees)
* Facilitate presentations for their nominees (or coordinate facilitator)

**During Award (3.1-3.2):**
* Prepare notifications for their nominees
* Send notifications to their nominees
* Coordinate publication materials for their nominees (if winners/finalists)

**Distribution:**
* Each Nominee Coach is assigned equal number of nominees from each category (Small, Medium, Large)
* Ensures balanced workload and category expertise

---

### Assessors (Volunteers - Stage 1)
**Responsibility:** Stage 1 evaluation

**Key Tasks:**
* Review assigned nominations (2.1)
* Check for COI and self-report (2.1)
* Verify eligibility and completeness (2.1)
* Score using Evaluation Framework (2.1)
* Provide comments and evidence notes (2.1)
* Help prepare nominees for Stage 2 (if requested by Nominee Coach)

---

### Judging Panel (Stage 2)
**Responsibility:** Final evaluation and winner selection

**Key Tasks:**
* Attend presentations (2.2)
* Score finalists independently (2.2)
* Ask questions during Q&A (2.2)
* Participate in outlier moderation (2.2)
* Deliberate on winners (2.2)

---

### Communications Coordinator
**Responsibility:** Centralized communications and publications

**Key Tasks:**
* Coordinate overall communication strategy
* Generate award letters/certificates (3.2)
* Prepare publication materials (3.2)
* Publish award results on websites (3.2)
* Handle post-award activities (3.2)
* Support Nominee Coaches with communication templates

---

## External Roles

### Applicant / Nominee
**Responsibility:** Submit nomination package

**Key Tasks:**
* Complete written submission (1.1)
* Upload evidence artifacts (1.1)
* Obtain Client Assessment Form (1.1)
* Submit nomination (1.1)
* Work with assigned Nominee Coach throughout process
* Prepare Stage 2 presentation if shortlisted (2.2)

---

### Client Representative
**Responsibility:** Complete Client Assessment Form

**Key Tasks:**
* Complete Client Assessment Form (1.1)
* Rate criteria and calculate scores (1.1)
* Sign and provide consent (1.1)

## RACI by Process Step

### 1.1 Nomination Submission
* **Responsible:** Applicant
* **Accountable:** Nominee Coach (assigned at submission)
* **Consulted:** Project Manager (for coach assignment)
* **Informed:** Project Manager

### 1.2 Intake and Initial Validation
* **Responsible:** Nominee Coach
* **Accountable:** Project Manager
* **Consulted:** Other Nominee Coaches (for consistency)
* **Informed:** Applicant, Project Manager

### 2.1 Stage 1 – Volunteer Screening
* **Responsible:** Assessors
* **Accountable:** Nominee Coach (for their assigned nominees)
* **Consulted:** Other Nominee Coaches (for consistency), Project Manager (for block removal)
* **Informed:** Applicant, Nominee Coach

### 2.2 Stage 2 – Judging Panel
* **Responsible:** Judging Panel
* **Accountable:** Project Manager
* **Consulted:** Nominee Coach (for their nominees' presentations)
* **Informed:** Applicant, Nominee Coach

### 3.1 Winner Selection and Notification
* **Responsible:** Nominee Coach (notifications to their nominees)
* **Accountable:** Project Manager
* **Consulted:** Communications Coordinator, Judging Panel
* **Informed:** Applicant, Nominee Coach

### 3.2 Award Publication
* **Responsible:** Communications Coordinator
* **Accountable:** Project Manager
* **Consulted:** Nominee Coaches (for their nominees' materials)
* **Informed:** Applicant, Public

## Nominee Coach Assignment Model

**Assignment Process:**
1. Nominee submits nomination
2. Project Manager assigns a Nominee Coach
3. Assignment ensures:
   * Equal number of nominees per coach from each category (Small, Medium, Large)
   * Balanced workload across coaches
   * Category expertise match (if possible)

**Example Distribution (3 Coaches, 9 nominees):**
* Coach A: 1 Small, 1 Medium, 1 Large
* Coach B: 1 Small, 1 Medium, 1 Large
* Coach C: 1 Small, 1 Medium, 1 Large

**Benefits:**
* Personal relationship between coach and nominee
* Consistent guidance throughout process
* Better nominee experience
* Clear accountability
* Coaches can develop category expertise

---

## Role Assignment (Small Team)

**Core Team:**
1. Project Manager (1 person)
2. Nominee Coaches (2-4 people, depending on number of nominees)
3. Communications Coordinator (1 person)

**Plus Volunteers:**
* 2-3 Assessors per nomination (Stage 1)
* 3-5 Judging Panel members (Stage 2)

---

## Nominee Coach Responsibilities Summary

Each Nominee Coach is responsible for their assigned nominees from start to finish:

1. **Submission Phase:** Guide through submission, check completeness, verify eligibility
2. **Stage 1 Phase:** Coordinate assessment, assign assessors, moderate scores, notify of results
3. **Stage 2 Phase:** Prepare nominees for presentations, coordinate with assessors for preparation help, schedule presentations
4. **Award Phase:** Notify of results, coordinate publication materials

**Key Advantage:** Nominee has one consistent contact person throughout the entire process, building trust and ensuring smooth experience.

---

## Project Manager - Guidance Documents

The Project Manager is responsible for creating and maintaining guidance documents for each role:

**Required Guidance Documents:**
1. **Nominee Coach Guide** - Process steps, responsibilities, templates, escalation procedures
2. **Assessor Guide** - Evaluation Framework, scoring guidelines, COI procedures, preparation support
3. **Judging Panel Guide** - Presentation protocol, scoring guidelines, decision-making protocol
4. **Communications Coordinator Guide** - Notification templates, publication guidelines, embargo procedures

**Block Removal:**
* Identify obstacles preventing roles from performing their tasks
* Resolve conflicts or resource constraints
* Escalate to Programme Owner (Oliver) when needed
* Ensure smooth handoffs between process phases
* Support roles when they encounter issues

---

## Key Simplifications

1. **One role per nominee** - Nominee Coach handles everything for their assigned nominees
2. **No separate Intake/Assessment/Communication roles** - All handled by Nominee Coach
3. **Equal distribution** - Ensures fair workload and category balance
4. **Personal relationship** - Better nominee experience with consistent contact
5. **Assessor support** - Assessors can help prepare nominees for Stage 2 (coaching role)
6. **Central coordination** - Project Manager provides guidance and removes blocks

---

_This model provides a personal, guided experience for each nominee while keeping the RACI structure simple and clear. The Project Manager ensures all roles have the guidance they need and obstacles are removed quickly._
