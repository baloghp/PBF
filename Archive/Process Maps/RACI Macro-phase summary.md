# RACI (Macro-phase summary)

Cover: Expected **individuals/groups** accountability. This document defines all roles required for the PBF Contractor of the Year program, including their responsibilities, accountabilities, and key activities. Roles are defined at the function level (not individual names) to accommodate a limited team where individuals may hold multiple roles.

---

## Role Definitions

### Executive and Strategic Roles

**Programme Owner**

* **Accountability:** Accountable for the overall success and outcomes of the PBF Contractor of the Year program
* **Key Responsibilities:**

    * Strategic oversight and decision-making authority
    * Final approval of winners and award decisions
    * Approval of key policies (COI, data protection, appeals)
    * Resource allocation and budget approval
    * Final deciding vote in case of stalemate (or delegates to designated authority)
    

**Process Owner**

* **Accountability:** Accountable for the end-to-end operational workflow (refer to Process Map)
* **Key Responsibilities:**

    * Design, document, and maintain the complete process workflow
    * Ensure process compliance and consistency
    * Monitor process performance against SLAs
    * Identify process improvements and implement changes
    * Coordinate handoffs between phases
    

---

### Operational Leadership Roles

**Intake Lead**

* **Accountability:** Accountable for managing the intake and initial validation of all nominations
* **Key Responsibilities:**

    * Manage nomination submission process
    * Conduct completeness checks on all submissions
    * Perform eligibility screening
    * Manage application lifecycle visibility
    * Handle incomplete applications and follow-ups
    * Respond to applicant questions within SLA
    * Assign eligible nominations to Stage 1 assessors
    

**Assessment Lead**

* **Accountability:** Accountable for managing the assessment process (both Stage 1 and Stage 2)
* **Key Responsibilities:**

    * Manage Stage 1 volunteer screening and evaluation
    * Assign nominations to assessors based on expertise and workload
    * Coordinate Stage 2 judging panel activities
    * Ensure COI checks and recusals are properly managed
    * Monitor assessment quality and inter-rater reliability
    * Manage shortlisting process
    

**Communications Lead**

* **Accountability:** Accountable for all internal and external communications
* **Key Responsibilities:**

    * Manage all communications with applicants (notifications, feedback, questions)
    * Prepare and send shortlist notifications
    * Prepare and send award notifications (winners, finalists, non-finalists)
    * Manage publication materials (case stories, press releases, social media)
    * Coordinate award ceremony communications
    

---

### Evaluation Roles

**Assessor** (Volunteer - Stage 1)

* **Responsibility:** Responsible for conducting Stage 1 screening and evaluation
* **Key Responsibilities:**

    * Review assigned nominations for eligibility and completeness
    * Check for COI and report immediately if identified
    * Apply Evaluation Framework (0-5 scale) independently
    * Score all criteria with weights
    * Provide per-criterion comments and evidence notes
    * Complete assessments within SLA
    
* **Skills:** Technical, specialist, or compliance expertise; understanding of evaluation criteria; analytical thinking; objectivity

**Moderator**

* **Responsibility:** Responsible for moderating assessment scores and ensuring consistency
* **Key Responsibilities:**

    * Review inter-rater reliability across assessors
    * Identify significant discrepancies (>2 points from median)
    * Facilitate moderation discussions on outliers
    * Calculate moderated average scores
    * Ensure scoring consistency across categories
    

**Judging Panel**

* **Responsibility:** Responsible for final evaluation and winner selection
* **Key Responsibilities:**

    * Attend Stage 2 presentations and Q&A sessions
    * Independently score finalists using Evaluation Framework (0-5 scale)
    * Ask questions during Q&A using standardized template
    * Participate in outlier moderation discussions
    * Participate in deliberation on winners
    * Maintain objectivity and COI-free status
    
* **Composition:** High-profile experts (e.g., PMI community leaders) with relevant domain mix; COI/recusal enforced

**Facilitator**

* **Responsibility:** Responsible for facilitating Stage 2 judging panel presentations
* **Key Responsibilities:**

    * Manage presentation sessions (time tracking, introductions)
    * Facilitate Q&A sessions using standardized question template
    * Record responses during Q&A
    * Manage recusals if COI arises during presentations
    * Moderate outlier discussions
    * Verify quorum before decisions
    * Ensure decision-making protocol is followed

### Platform and Technical Roles

**Platform Administrator / Technical Lead**

* **Accountability:** Accountable for platform selection, setup, configuration, and technical operations
* **Key Responsibilities:**

    * Lead platform evaluation and selection process
    * Configure nomination platform (categories, criteria, weights, scoring scale)
    * Set up workflows and automation
    * Configure email templates and notifications
    * Set up Client Assessment Form collection system
    * Configure QR code generation
    * Manage platform user accounts and permissions
    * Monitor platform performance and uptime
    * Provide technical support to users
    * Manage data security and privacy settings
    

**Program Secretariat**

* **Responsibility:** Responsible for administrative support and COI management
* **Key Responsibilities:**

    * Document COI declarations and recusals
    * Maintain audit trail of all COI-related activities
    * Assign alternate assessors when recusals occur
    * Verify quorum after recusals
    * Maintain COI disclosure tracking system
    * Support Process Owner with administrative tasks
    * Maintain program records and documentation
    

---

### External Roles

**Applicant / Nominee**

* **Responsibility:** Responsible for submitting complete nomination packages
* **Key Responsibilities:**

    * Complete written submission (≤2,000 words)
    * Upload all required evidence artifacts
    * Obtain and submit Client Assessment Form from client
    * Provide accurate information
    * Accept publication consent
    * Prepare and deliver Stage 2 presentation (if shortlisted)
    * **Designate a contact person** who will serve as the primary point of contact for PBF throughout the nomination process
    

**Client Representative**

* **Responsibility:** Responsible for completing Client Assessment Form
* **Key Responsibilities:**

    * Complete Client Assessment Form accurately
    * Rate 7 criteria (0-10 scale)
    * Calculate scores correctly
    * Provide evidence notes for ratings ≥8 or ≤4
    * Sign and provide consent for evaluation and publication
    

**Contact Person (Nominee Side)**

* **Responsibility:** Serves as the primary point of contact for the nominee organization
* **Key Responsibilities:**

    * Act as the "gatekeeper" for all communications between nominee and PBF
    * Respond to questions and requests from PBF contact person
    * Coordinate information gathering within the nominee organization
    * Ensure timely responses to PBF inquiries
    * Facilitate communication flow
    

**Contact Person (PBF Side)**

* **Responsibility:** Serves as the primary point of contact for PBF
* **Key Responsibilities:**

    * Act as the "gatekeeper" for all communications between PBF and nominees
    * Respond to questions and concerns from nominees
    * Provide guidance and support throughout the nomination process
    * Coordinate with other PBF team members as needed
    * Ensure nominees know whom to contact for any issues or questions

## Multi-Role Considerations

Given the limited team size, individuals may hold multiple roles. Key considerations:

1. **COI Management:** Individuals cannot assess nominations where they have conflicts
2. **Separation of Duties:**

    * Assessment Lead should not be an Assessor for same nominations
    * Programme Owner should not be an Assessor
    * Process Owner can support but not make final decisions
    
3. **Workload Management:** Distribute roles to balance workload
4. **Skills Alignment:** Assign roles based on individual skills and expertise

**Recommended Role Combinations (if needed):**

* Process Owner + Intake Lead (operational focus)
* Assessment Lead + Moderator (evaluation focus)
* Communications Lead + Program Secretariat (administrative focus)
* Platform Administrator (standalone technical role)
* Contact Person (PBF Side) can be combined with Intake Lead or Communications Lead

## Training Requirements

### Mandatory Training for All Roles:

* COI Policy and Procedures
* Data Privacy and Protection
* Process Overview

### Role-Specific Training:

* **Assessors:** Evaluation Framework, Scoring Guidelines, T&M Contract Evaluation
* **Judging Panel Members:** Evaluation Framework, Presentation Protocol, Decision-Making Protocol
* **Facilitator:** Presentation Management, Q&A Protocol, Outlier Moderation
* **Intake Lead:** Eligibility Criteria, Completeness Checks, Platform Usage
* **Platform Administrator:** Platform Configuration, System Administration, Data Security
* **Communications Lead:** Notification Templates, Publication Guidelines, Embargo Management
* **Contact Person (PBF Side):** Communication protocols, escalation procedures, nominee support

---

## Notes for Role Assignment

1. **Start with Core Roles:** Assign Programme Owner, Process Owner, and Platform Administrator first
2. **Build Operational Team:** Add Intake Lead, Assessment Lead, Communications Lead
3. **Assign Contact Person:** Designate PBF Contact Person early in the process
4. **Recruit Volunteers:** Recruit Assessors and Judging Panel Members
5. **Cross-Train:** Ensure backup coverage for critical roles
6. **Document Assignments:** Maintain a role assignment log
7. **Review Regularly:** Review role assignments and workload quarterly
