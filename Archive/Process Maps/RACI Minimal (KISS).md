# RACI - Minimal (KISS Approach)

**Principle:** Keep It Simple - Only define roles that are essential for the process to function.

---

## Core Roles (Essential)

### 1. Programme Owner
**Accountability:** Final decision authority, overall program success

**Key Activities:**
* Final approval of winners
* Final deciding vote in stalemate
* Strategic oversight

---

### 2. Process Lead
**Accountability:** End-to-end process operations

**Key Activities:**
* Manage intake and validation (completeness, eligibility)
* Assign nominations to assessors
* Coordinate assessment process
* Manage platform/technical setup
* Handle COI documentation (simple tracking)

**Note:** Combines Process Owner + Intake Lead + Platform Administrator + Program Secretariat functions

---

### 3. Assessment Lead
**Accountability:** Assessment process (Stage 1 & Stage 2)

**Key Activities:**
* Assign nominations to assessors
* Manage Stage 1 screening
* Coordinate Stage 2 judging panel
* Moderate scoring discrepancies
* Facilitate Stage 2 presentations
* Manage shortlisting

**Note:** Combines Assessment Lead + Moderator + Facilitator functions

---

### 4. Communications Lead
**Accountability:** All communications

**Key Activities:**
* All applicant communications (questions, notifications)
* Shortlist notifications
* Award notifications (winners, finalists)
* Publication materials
* Contact person for nominees

**Note:** Combines Communications Lead + Contact Person (PBF Side) functions

---

### 5. Assessors (Volunteers - Stage 1)
**Responsibility:** Stage 1 evaluation

**Key Activities:**
* Review nominations
* Check COI (self-report)
* Score using Evaluation Framework
* Complete assessments

---

### 6. Judging Panel (Stage 2)
**Responsibility:** Final evaluation and winner selection

**Key Activities:**
* Attend presentations
* Score finalists
* Participate in Q&A
* Deliberate on winners

---

## External Roles

### Applicant / Nominee
**Responsibility:** Submit nomination package

### Client Representative
**Responsibility:** Complete Client Assessment Form

---

## RACI by Phase

### SUBMISSION
* **Responsible:** Applicant
* **Accountable:** Process Lead
* **Consulted:** Communications Lead
* **Informed:** Programme Owner

### ASSESSMENT (Stage 1)
* **Responsible:** Assessors
* **Accountable:** Assessment Lead
* **Consulted:** Process Lead
* **Informed:** Applicants, Communications Lead

### ASSESSMENT (Stage 2)
* **Responsible:** Judging Panel
* **Accountable:** Assessment Lead / Programme Owner
* **Consulted:** Assessment Lead (facilitation)
* **Informed:** Applicants, Communications Lead

### AWARD
* **Responsible:** Communications Lead
* **Accountable:** Programme Owner
* **Consulted:** Assessment Lead, Judging Panel
* **Informed:** Applicants, Public

---

## Role Combinations (Small Team)

Given limited team size, one person can hold multiple roles:

**Recommended combinations:**
* Process Lead (standalone - operational focus)
* Assessment Lead (standalone - evaluation focus)
* Communications Lead (standalone - communication focus)
* Programme Owner (can combine with strategic oversight, but not assessment)

**Cannot combine:**
* Assessment Lead cannot be Assessor for same nominations
* Programme Owner cannot be Assessor
* Judging Panel must be independent

---

## Simplified Role Matrix

| Role | Core Function | Can Combine With |
|------|--------------|------------------|
| Programme Owner | Final decisions | Strategic oversight only |
| Process Lead | Operations | None (too much work) |
| Assessment Lead | Evaluation | None (too much work) |
| Communications Lead | Communications | None (too much work) |
| Assessors | Stage 1 scoring | Volunteers (independent) |
| Judging Panel | Stage 2 decisions | Independent experts |

---

## What Was Removed (Simplified)

1. **Process Owner** → Merged into Process Lead
2. **Intake Lead** → Merged into Process Lead
3. **Platform Administrator** → Merged into Process Lead
4. **Program Secretariat** → Merged into Process Lead (simple COI tracking)
5. **Moderator** → Assessment Lead does this
6. **Facilitator** → Assessment Lead does this
7. **Contact Person (PBF Side)** → Communications Lead does this
8. **Contact Person (Nominee Side)** → Not needed (nominee designates internally)

---

## Minimum Team Size

**Core Team (4 people):**
1. Programme Owner
2. Process Lead
3. Assessment Lead
4. Communications Lead

**Plus Volunteers:**
* 2-3 Assessors per nomination (Stage 1)
* 3-5 Judging Panel members (Stage 2)

---

_This minimal RACI focuses on essential roles only. Additional roles can be added later if needed, but this structure supports the complete process._
