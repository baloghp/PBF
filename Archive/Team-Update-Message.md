## 🎯 Award Credo & What Makes Winners Exemplary

I've tried to define what makes our winners truly exemplary, not just competent. This is can be the guiding principle for the award. **Please review and let me know your thoughts.**

### ✅ Award Credo Established

**Added to main project page:** [Project Contractor Of The Year Award](https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/97419265)

**The Credo:**
* **Beyond competence → excellence** - Not just "did the job well," but did something uniquely valuable
* **Beyond compliance → impact** - Not just "had good processes," but created measurable, lasting change
* **Beyond project success → industry contribution** - Not just "client was happy," but advanced the profession and shared knowledge
* **Beyond standard practice → innovation** - Not just "followed best practices," but created new best practices
* **Beyond completion → legacy** - Not just "finished the project," but left lasting impact and transformed relationships

**The deciding factor:** Would we want to publish this as a case study? Would others learn from this? Is this truly exemplary, not just competent?

### ✅ Submission Package Enhanced - Three New Required Sections

**Major change:** Added three new required sections to distinguish exemplary winners from competent performers.

**New Required Sections:**

1. **"What Makes This Exemplary?" (500 words - Required)**
   - What sets this apart from a "good" project?
   - Innovation & Uniqueness
   - Broader Impact
   - Industry Contribution
   - **Evaluated as part of:** "Innovation & Industry Advancement" criterion

2. **"Impact Measurement & Transformation" (300 words - Required)**
   - Quantifiable outcomes (specific metrics)
   - Transformation evidence
   - Long-term benefits (6-12 months after completion)
   - ROI or value demonstration
   - **Evaluated as part of:** "Project success (outcomes)" criterion

3. **"Lessons Learned & Industry Contribution" (300 words - Required)**
   - What did you learn?
   - Knowledge sharing (publications, presentations, templates, mentoring)
   - Industry contribution
   - Replicability
   - **Evaluated as part of:** "Innovation & Industry Advancement" criterion

**Updated in:** [Submission Package](https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/98598913)

### ✅ Evaluation Framework Enhanced

**New Criterion Added:** "Innovation & Industry Advancement" (0-5 scale)

This criterion evaluates what makes the project exemplary and distinguishes winners from competent performers:
- Innovation & Uniqueness
- Industry Contribution
- Broader Impact
- Case Study Worthiness

**Performance Descriptors:**
- **4-5 (Very Good to Excellent):** Significant innovation, industry-relevant, active knowledge sharing, replicable practices, case study worthy
- **Winners should typically score 4-5** on this criterion

**Updated in:** [Evaluation Framework](https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/98631681)

**Impact:** This ensures we're recognizing truly exemplary projects that advance the profession, not just competent ones that completed successfully.
