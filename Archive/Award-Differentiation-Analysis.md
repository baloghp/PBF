# Award Differentiation Analysis: Making Winners Truly Exemplary

**Date:** December 4, 2025  
**Purpose:** Identify what makes award winners truly exemplary and how to differentiate the PBF Contractor of the Year Award

---

## Current State Analysis

### What We're Currently Asking For (Standard Documentation)

1. **Written submission** (≤2,000 words) - Standard narrative
2. **Evidence artifacts** - Contract matrix, RACI, metrics, code of conduct
3. **Client Assessment Form** - Client reference/rating
4. **Standard evaluation criteria** - Project success, governance, risk management, etc.

### The Problem

**This is what every award asks for.** We're collecting standard documentation that any competent contractor should have. What makes a winner **truly exemplary** beyond just having good documentation?

---

## Research Findings: What Makes Award Winners Stand Out

Based on analysis of successful business awards, the following factors distinguish exemplary winners:

### 1. **Compelling Narrative & Storytelling**
- Winners tell a **story**, not just list achievements
- They show the journey: challenge → innovation → transformation → impact
- Judges remember stories, not data sheets

### 2. **Measurable Impact Beyond Project Success**
- Not just "project completed on time/budget"
- **Quantifiable outcomes:** Efficiency gains, cost savings, quality improvements
- **Long-term impact:** How the project transformed the client's business
- **Industry impact:** How it advanced the field

### 3. **Innovation & Uniqueness**
- What did they do **differently**?
- Novel approaches, pioneering techniques
- Challenged industry norms
- Created new best practices

### 4. **Broader Impact & Legacy**
- **Industry contribution:** Did they share knowledge? Create templates? Influence others?
- **Sustainability:** Environmental, social, economic sustainability
- **Community engagement:** Beyond the project scope
- **Thought leadership:** Publications, speaking, mentoring

### 5. **Cultural Transformation**
- How did they transform the client-contractor relationship?
- Did they change how the client works?
- Did they create lasting organizational change?

### 6. **Lessons Learned & Knowledge Sharing**
- What did they learn that benefits the industry?
- How are they sharing this knowledge?
- Are they contributing to the profession?

---

## Recommendations: What Should Make Our Winners Exemplary

### Option 1: Add "Exemplary Factor" Section (Recommended)

Add a new required section to the submission that asks:

**"What Makes This Project Exemplary?"** (500 words)

Nominees must explain:
- **What sets this apart** from a "good" project?
- **Innovation or uniqueness** - What did you do differently?
- **Broader impact** - Beyond project success, what changed?
- **Industry contribution** - How does this advance the field?
- **Legacy** - What lasting impact remains?

**Evaluation:** This becomes a **deciding factor** in Stage 2. Judges ask: "Would we want to publish this as a case study? Would others learn from this?"

---

### Option 2: Enhance "Lessons Learned" Section

Currently "lessons learned" is just a bullet point. Make it more prominent:

**"Lessons Learned & Industry Contribution"** (300 words)

Ask specifically:
- What did you learn that others should know?
- How are you sharing this knowledge? (Publications, presentations, templates)
- What would you do differently next time?
- How has this project influenced your approach to future work?

**Evaluation:** Winners should demonstrate **knowledge sharing** and **thought leadership**.

---

### Option 3: Add "Impact Measurement" Section

Beyond standard metrics, ask for:

**"Measurable Impact & Transformation"** (300 words)

Require:
- **Quantifiable outcomes:** Specific metrics (e.g., "reduced processing time by 40%", "saved client £X million")
- **Transformation evidence:** How did the project change the client's business?
- **Long-term benefits:** What impact remains 6-12 months after completion?
- **ROI or value demonstration:** Beyond contract value, what value was created?

**Evaluation:** Winners should show **measurable, lasting impact**, not just completion.

---

### Option 4: Add "Innovation & Uniqueness" Criterion

Add as a new evaluation criterion (weighted):

**"Innovation & Industry Advancement"** (0-5 scale)

Evaluates:
- Novel approaches or techniques
- Challenging industry norms
- Creating new best practices
- Advancing the profession
- Replicability for others

**Evaluation:** Winners should score 4-5 on innovation, demonstrating they did something **uniquely valuable**.

---

### Option 5: Require "Case Study Readiness"

Add requirement:

**"Case Study Potential"**

Nominees must demonstrate:
- Story worth telling (compelling narrative)
- Lessons worth sharing
- Replicable practices
- Measurable impact
- Industry relevance

**Evaluation:** Only projects that would make **valuable case studies** can win. This ensures winners are truly exemplary and worth publishing.

---

## Recommended Approach: Combination

### Phase 1: Enhance Submission Package (Immediate)

Add to written submission requirements:

1. **"What Makes This Exemplary?"** (500 words) - Required section
   - What sets this apart?
   - Innovation/uniqueness
   - Broader impact
   - Industry contribution

2. **Enhanced "Lessons Learned"** (300 words)
   - What did you learn?
   - How are you sharing knowledge?
   - Industry contribution

3. **"Impact Measurement"** (300 words)
   - Quantifiable outcomes
   - Transformation evidence
   - Long-term benefits

### Phase 2: Add Evaluation Criterion (For Next Cycle)

Add to Evaluation Framework:

**"Innovation & Industry Advancement"** (0-5 scale, weighted)

- 0-1: Standard approach, no innovation
- 2-3: Some novel elements, minor improvements
- 4: Significant innovation, industry-relevant
- 5: Groundbreaking approach, advances the profession, replicable

### Phase 3: Stage 2 Presentation Enhancement

In Stage 2 presentations, require nominees to address:

**"Why Should You Win?"**

- What makes you exemplary?
- What can others learn from you?
- How does this advance the profession?

## What Makes Winners Truly Exemplary: The Deciding Factors

Based on research, winners should demonstrate:

### 1. **Beyond Competence → Excellence**
- Not just "did the job well"
- **Did something uniquely valuable**

### 2. **Beyond Documentation → Impact**
- Not just "had good processes"
- **Created measurable, lasting change**

### 3. **Beyond Project Success → Industry Contribution**
- Not just "client was happy"
- **Advanced the profession, shared knowledge**

### 4. **Beyond Standard Practice → Innovation**
- Not just "followed best practices"
- **Created new best practices**

### 5. **Beyond Completion → Legacy**
- Not just "finished the project"
- **Left lasting impact, transformed relationships**
