## Clip 3 — The time you save gets filled

**Speakers:** Peter Balogh, then Dr. Kris Lea

### Keep 1 — Peter — 12:07–13:16

**In:** 12:07, on “Let me give you an example.”  
**Out:** 13:16, on “They can schedule two audits per day.”

**Words**

Let me give you an example. We have a lot of auditors here. We are doing audits of buildings, for example. We created a tool for them so that writing the audit reports is easy.

Usually the way these guys were operating is one day, one audit. Why? Because in the morning they went out, in the afternoon they came back, and then they spent the rest of the day typing the report, organizing the images, typing everything up.

Now we gave them a tool. On your mobile phone you go, you click, you speak, you go, you click, you speak — and by the time you finish, you have a full report. You go back after lunch, you have a full report. So what does this mean? They can schedule two audits per day.
