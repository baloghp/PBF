## The job of this post

Make the award feel like something a contractor would *want* to survive, not just something a client fills in. The judgement process itself is the product: independent assessors, a client who rates you without you holding the pen, a short published case if you place. That is marketing you cannot write yourself. A seal of quality that is not a brochure.

## Angle (one idea only)

Self-written excellence is cheap. What holds in the next RFP is proof that went through somebody else's room.
