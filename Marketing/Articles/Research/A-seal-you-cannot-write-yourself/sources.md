## What the judgement process actually produces (internal, no new statistic)

From [Nominee Guide](../../../Contractor_of_The_Year/Guides/Contractor-of-the-Year-Nominee-Guide-2026.md) and [Evaluation Framework](../../../Confluence/Project-Contractor-Of-The-Year-Award/98631681%20-%20Evaluation%20Framework.md):

- At least **two independent assessors** score the packet on the nine criteria
- **Assessor credential gate is hard:** PMP **or** PBP, no exceptions, no UAT grandfathering. The applicant supplies the number and the team verifies it (PMP via public Credly badge URL, PBP via the PBF certification registry) before a Stage 1 seat is granted. Sources: [assessor EOI landing](../../../Contractor_of_The_Year/Implementation/Assessor-Wix/01-eoi-landing.md) ("PMP or PBP. That is the gate. You give us the number, we verify it."), [Assessor Recruitment Strategy](../../../Contractor_of_The_Year/Working%20Folder/2026-07-12%20Sprint/Assessor-Recruitment-Strategy.md)
- Assessors must **record the evidence** behind each criterion score
- **Customer evaluation** is a unique link emailed to the client; nominee cannot fill it in, forward it, or upload it
- Publication of a **short case profile** if overall winner, category first place, or one of the three recognised places in category
- Categories assigned at intake (Small / Medium / Large by headcount). Do not make this post a category explainer
