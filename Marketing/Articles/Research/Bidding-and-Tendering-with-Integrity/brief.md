## Angle (one idea only)

Everyone reads bidding integrity as a promise about the work. There is a second promise in the same document that nobody audits: that the price can be delivered without financing it off the firms below you. The tell arrives months later, in the payment run.

## Shape

1. Integrity in a bid is normally understood as not overstating the team and not hiding the assumption that makes the number work. True, but that is a promise about the work.
2. The second promise: this price does not require somebody smaller to wait for their money.
3. Evidence that this is where it breaks: late payment, retentions held as working capital rather than ring-fenced, upstream insolvency, construction top of the insolvency table.
4. The constructive turn: project bank accounts already solve it. Ring-fenced, trust status, tiers 2 and 3 paid simultaneously in 3-5 days.
5. The provocation: the objections are more interesting than the mechanism. An honest price loses nothing by having the supply chain paid on time.
6. Award tie, light: this is one of the nine, and it is the question no vendor scorecard asks.
