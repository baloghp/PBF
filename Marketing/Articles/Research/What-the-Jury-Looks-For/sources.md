# Sources — "What the jury looks for"

Internal only. Do not paste the rubric into the public post.

## The nine criteria (Evaluation Framework, still draft)

From [Evaluation Framework](../../../Confluence/Project-Contractor-Of-The-Year-Award/98631681%20-%20Evaluation%20Framework.md) and [Assessor Guide](../../../Confluence/Project-Contractor-Of-The-Year-Award/130056195%20-%20Assessor%20Guide.md):

1. Project Success (outcomes)
2. Agility & Adaptability
3. Commercial Model (liquidity) — *unique to Project Business*
4. Legal Soundness
5. Interface and Governance
6. Risk Management (cross-corporate)
7. People Development
8. Team & Business Acumen (integrity and conduct)
9. **Innovation & Industry Advancement** — novelty, knowledge sharing, replicability, case-study worth

Stage 2 [Judging Panel Guide](../../../Confluence/Project-Contractor-Of-The-Year-Award/129662987%20-%20Judging%20Panel%20Guide.md): "Focus strongly on Criterion 9."

## The public-safe line (use this, not the table)

From the framework, "What Makes Winners Truly Exemplary?":

> The deciding factor: Would we want to publish this as a case study? Would others learn from this? Is this truly exemplary, not just competent?

Publication: winner + top 3 finalists get a short case story (subject to consent) — [Governance & Jury Protocol](../../../Confluence/Project-Contractor-Of-The-Year-Award/97779722%20-%20Governance%20%26%20Jury%20Protocol.md).
