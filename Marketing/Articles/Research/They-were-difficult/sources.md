## Headline metric

PMI, *The Essential Role of Communications* (Pulse in-depth): of US$135 million at risk per US$1 billion spent, **56% (US$75 million)** is at risk due to **ineffective communications**. One-half of unsuccessful projects related to ineffective communications.

- https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/documents/public/pdf/learning/thought-leadership/pulse/the-essential-role-of-communications.pdf

Safe rephrase: more than half the money sitting at risk on projects is tied to communication that did not work, which in this meeting usually means the conversation that never happened.

Pulse 2021 appendix, supporting only: inadequate/poor communication named by **28%** as a primary cause of project failure. Do not stack it on 56%. One number.
