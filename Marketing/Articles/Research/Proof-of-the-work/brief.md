## The job of this post

Raise interest in the award with the smallest possible argument. Your certificate proves you know the work. It does not prove your work. In a market where a few hundred thousand people earn the same letters every year, the scarce thing is a project somebody outside your firm has examined. That is what the award produces. So nominate.

## Angle (one idea only)

The letters are not a differentiator any more. Proof of the work is.
