# Sources — "Double proof"

Fact-checked 1 Oct 2026. The post uses **one** number. The video uses none.

## The one number

**79 percent of proposal teams used generative AI on RFP work in 2025.**

Loopio, 2026 RFP Response Trends & Benchmarks Report. Survey of **1,533 response management professionals** across 17+ industries. Press release, 11 Mar 2026: "Nearly 80% of teams used generative AI in 2025, up 10 percentage points from the year prior." The report page and Loopio's own statistics round-up both say **79%**, up from 68% the year before.

- https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260311267176/en/New-Loopio-Research-Finds-AI-Adoption-and-Leadership-Expectations-Are-Climbing-as-RFP-Revenue-Influence-Reaches-a-Five-Year-High
- https://loopio.com/trends-report/
- https://loopio.com/blog/rfp-statistics-win-rates/

**Wording that is safe.** "Loopio surveyed the teams who write proposals. Seventy-nine percent had used generative AI on the work in 2025."

**Say it is Loopio's survey.** They sell response software, and the sample is proposal teams, not project contractors. One clause is enough. Do not write "79 percent of contractors" or "the industry found."

## Held back

Responsive, *Inside the Buyer's Mind* (July 2025, n = 350 B2B buyers at organisations issuing at least 10 RFPs a year): **18 percent** already use AI tools to tell whether a vendor drafted the RFP response with AI. The blog rounds this to "1 in 5." The report says 18 percent, 52 percent in APAC, 8 percent in the US. A second number would crowd the three video lines.

- Report PDF: https://media.trustradius.com/product-downloadables/9W/43/Q1VIJTH1XOOG.pdf
- Blog: https://www.responsive.io/blog/what-buyers-look-for-rfp
