## Events listing (paste into the workshop)

**Title:** Meet PCotY — why this award, who it is for, and how to take part

**Short (about 50 words):**

The Project Contractor of the Year Award is about to open its first public cycle. This hour covers why the award exists, what a nomination actually is, how Stage 1 and Stage 2 work on one shared field, and what we ask of assessors. Four awards: Overall, Small, Medium, Large. Nominations open 1 September. Assessor applications close 15 October.

**Full description (paste as the workshop summary):**

Most awards in project work celebrate the manager on the customer side. The contractor who delivered the work is usually a footnote. Project Contractor of the Year exists to name that other half of the relationship: contracted delivery across a customer-contractor boundary, scored on evidence, not reputation — a seal the firm cannot write itself.

This first-cycle workshop is for people who might nominate a contractor (including more than one project), complete a client evaluation, or sit as a Stage 1 assessor.

In one hour we will cover:

1. Why the award exists, and what "excellence" means here (not just a finished job) — candour, courage, foresight; the difficult contractor and the unnoticed one; would we publish this as a case study?
2. Who can be nominated, what the packet looks like, and why the client's own evaluation is required.
3. How the cycle runs: nominations 1 September to 31 October (you may enter more than one project). Stage 1 and Stage 2 judge every project the same way. The panel names four awards — one Overall winner plus one in Small, Medium, and Large. Winners in January 2027.
4. The assessor job: PMP or PBP, a short calibration, 3-5 nominations scored remotely in November and December.

Bring a name. If you already know a contractor whose work should be on the table, this hour will tell you what to do next. If you hold PMP or PBP and might assess, bring that too.

Nominations: [ittd.space/pbf-pcoty](https://www.ittd.space/pbf-pcoty)  
Become an assessor: [ittd.space/become-an-assessor](https://www.ittd.space/become-an-assessor)

## What success looks like

This sitting is a launch briefing, not calibration and not a how-to-win clinic. Those are W2 (mid-September) and the assessor orientation later.

Walk away with:

- At least a few people who will nominate, or will ask a client to be ready
- At least a few PMP/PBP applications started
- A recording Oliver can clip for LinkedIn

Do not try to teach all nine criteria in depth. Name them as questions, land Criterion 9, point to the guides.
