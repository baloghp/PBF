## Decision

Remove the **Nominee Coach** volunteer role. Replace intake with an **automated pre-screening agent** (eligibility + completeness + attachments only — not evaluation). **Assessors and judges stay human.**

Operational duties move to **Project Manager / Award Operations** (assessor assignment, COI, moderation, shortlist) and **Communications Coordinator** (nominee notifications). Archive the Coach Guide; do not delete.

**Conventions (see full record):** Pre-screen flags gaps → Comms sends templated missing-item email; assessor quorum **≥2** (target 3); role title **Communications Coordinator**.
