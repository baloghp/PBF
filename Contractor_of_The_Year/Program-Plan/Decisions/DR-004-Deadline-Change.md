## Decision

For the P-CotY 2026 cycle:

- Nominations close **30 November 2026**.
- The customer evaluation for each nomination closes on the same date, **30 November 2026**.
- Assessor expressions of interest close **15 November 2026**.
- Finalists are announced **20 December 2026** as a milestone on the overall public timeline.

The nomination window still opens **1 September 2026**. Stage 2, winner announcement, and ceremony dates are unchanged and their feasibility against this schedule was confirmed under R7 on 2026-10-04.

## Context

The nomination deadline is being extended by one month, with the finalist announcement moved to 20 December. The assessor recruitment cutoff also moves from 15 October to 15 November. No individual finalist or applicant outcome communications have been sent, so this decision does not require individual date-change notices.

## Consequences

- The nomination and client evaluation deadlines must be treated as one synchronized cutoff in platform settings, participant guidance, and communications.
- Marketing and nomination acquisition continue through November.
- Intake, eligibility review, assessor assignment, scoring, moderation, shortlist approval, and finalist announcement follow the confirmed critical path below. Assessor capacity, volunteer availability, holiday coverage, and the January/February schedule were confirmed by the user on 2026-10-04 (R5–R7).
- The 15 November assessor EOI cutoff is a Sunday and is only 15 days before nominations close. WS2/WS4 confirmed the credential checks, roster, onboarding, calibration, and assessor capacity under R5 on 2026-10-04.
- The former assumption of a shortlist and clean pause before Christmas is no longer a planning constraint. December availability and holiday-period coverage, including the Sunday announcement, were confirmed under R6 on 2026-10-04.
- Historical meeting notes and sent communications remain records; maintained plans and future-facing materials must use this decision.
