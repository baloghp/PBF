## Decision

Run the first cycle as **"P-CotY 2026"** with a split schedule:

- **Stage 1 (screening) completed in 2026**, with the shortlist locked **before the Christmas break**.
- **Stage 2 (judging panel) + decision + ceremony in late January 2027.**

We explicitly **reject** the alternative of compressing the whole cycle to finish before Christmas 2026.

## Context

- Today is ~6 months from a feasible ceremony date (decision made 2026-06-05).
- This is the **first-ever cycle** — a new idea, so volume and process maturity are unproven.
- The team is **volunteer-run** (limited, variable bandwidth; reduced availability in December).
- **No sponsors secured yet.**
- The award is largely **digital/virtual** (platform submissions, virtual Stage 2), so heavy event logistics (venue, catering, AV rigging) are minimal; the real new effort is outreach, comms, sponsorship, and critical-path discipline.

## Options Considered

### Option A — Stage 2 in January 2027 (CHOSEN)
Stage 1 in autumn 2026; shortlist before Christmas; Stage 2 + ceremony late January 2027; branded P-CotY 2026.

### Option B — Finish before Christmas 2026 (REJECTED)
Entire cycle compressed so the ceremony lands ~mid-December 2026.

## Comparison

| Factor | Option B (pre-Christmas, tight) | Option A (Stage 2 January) |
|---|---|---|
| Nomination window | Falls in European summer holidays (weak) | Sept–Oct, post-summer (strong engagement) |
| Volunteer load | Crunch through Nov–Dec | Paced; natural break after shortlist |
| Judge availability | December (very hard to book) | Mid/late January (good) |
| Sponsor runway | ~3 months (too short) | ~6 months to land 1–2 sponsors |
| Slack for a first cycle | None (zero float on immovable date) | Built-in buffer |
| Quality of first impression | At risk | Protected |

## Reasoning

1. **Backward planning from a mid-December ceremony forces the nomination drive into July/August** — the worst outreach window in Europe — while cold (no sponsors, volunteer team).
2. **It pushes judge scheduling into December**, the most congested month for high-profile jurors.
3. **It leaves zero slack** on a one-shot, immovable date run by volunteers whose December availability also dips.
4. **January protects quality**: post-summer nomination window, paced volunteer load, realistic judge booking, and ~6 months of sponsor runway.
5. **The holiday gap is placed at a natural seam** — finish Stage 1 and lock the shortlist before the break, pause cleanly, then resume for Stage 2 + ceremony in January. Avoids running work through Christmas and reduces volunteer burnout.

## Branding note

"P-CotY 2026, winners announced January 2027" is normal and defensible. The **award year refers to the eligibility/competition period, not the announcement date**. Comparable awards (incl. the PMI benchmark) routinely announce a cohort in the following calendar period.

## Consequences

- **WS3 (Marketing):** plan the nomination drive for September; do NOT rely on summer.
- **WS2 (Recruitment):** confirm judges for a January Stage 2 window; recruit **assessors** through **15 Oct** (no coach role — DR-002).
- **WS7 (Sponsorship):** run a **lean first edition** (digital certificates/recognition, perhaps one modest prize). Do not let the sponsor gap block launch. Use the completed cycle's results, winners, case stories, and reach metrics as the **proof-of-concept to sell multi-year sponsorship for 2027**.
- **WS1 (PMO):** build the dated critical-path timeline around the holiday seam; mirror as PBF-17 milestones.
- **WS6 (Ceremony):** target late January 2027; virtual format assumed pending DR on ceremony format.
