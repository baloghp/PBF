## Objective

Secure and prepare enough qualified, calibrated **assessors and judges** to run a full award cycle fairly and on schedule. *(Nominee Coach role dropped — see [DR-002](../Decisions/DR-002-Drop-Nominee-Coach.md).)*

## Scope

- In scope: role definitions, recruitment outreach, selection, onboarding, framework calibration/training, scheduling, COI declarations, retention.
- Out of scope: running the assessment process itself (WS4), COI policy authoring (WS5).

## Sub-tracks

- **2a. Assessors (Stage 1)** — volunteer recruitment and onboarding; **assignment per nomination is WS4/PM** (see [Roles and RACI](../../Confluence/Project-Contractor-Of-The-Year-Award/117964816 - Roles and RACI.md)).
- **2b. Judging Panel (Stage 2)** — high-profile jury; different ask, timeline, scheduling around finalist presentations.
- **2c. Onboarding & Training** — framework calibration, COI acknowledgment, platform training, retention.
