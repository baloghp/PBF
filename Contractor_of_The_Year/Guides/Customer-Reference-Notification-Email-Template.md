# Customer reference — notification email template

**For nominators** preparing a Project Contractor of the Year (PCoTY) 2027 submission.

Send this **before** you enter the customer representative on your nomination (or immediately after), so they know the evaluation email is legitimate and not phishing.

**Platform:** [ittd.space/pbf-pcoty](https://www.ittd.space/pbf-pcoty)  
**Customer guide (optional link for them):** [Contractor-of-the-Year-Customer-Guide-2026.md](Contractor-of-the-Year-Customer-Guide-2026.md)  
**Deadline:** customer evaluation and nomination both close **31 October 2026**.

---

## Before you send

- Use the customer's **current work email** — a wrong address fails silently; nobody is notified.
- Pick someone who still works on the account and still answers email.
- You **cannot** complete the customer form, forward the link, or upload it on their behalf.
- Replace every `[bracket]` below. Delete optional lines you do not need.

---

## Subject line (pick one)

- `PCoTY nomination — customer evaluation email coming from Project Business Foundation`
- `Heads-up: customer reference request for [Project name]`
- `Project Contractor of the Year — please expect an evaluation form`

---

## Email body

```
Dear [Customer first name / title + surname],

I am preparing a nomination for [Project name] for the Project Contractor of the
Year 2027 award, run by the Project Business Foundation (PBF).

As part of the submission, I have named you as the customer representative for
this project. The award requires an independent customer evaluation — your
perspective is a formal part of the nomination, not a reference letter we can
supply ourselves.

What happens next:

1. I enter your work email address on the nomination platform.
2. PBF sends you a separate email with a unique link to your evaluation form.
   I do not receive that link and cannot complete the form for you.
3. You rate the contractor on seven criteria (0–10 scale, with short notes where
   scores are very high or very low) and confirm publication consent.
4. The form must be completed by 31 October 2026 — the same close date as
   nominations.

I am writing now so the message from PBF does not catch you off guard or look
like phishing. If you receive an evaluation request and were not expecting it,
please contact me before opening any links.

Please reply to confirm:

- [Work email address] is still the best address to reach you at.
- You are willing to act as the customer representative for this nomination.
- [Optional: anyone else we should copy or loop in, e.g. procurement or PMO]

Thank you for your support. I appreciate the time.

Best regards,

[Your name]
[Your role]
[Your company]
[Your phone — optional]
```

## Short version (quick ping)

Use when you have already spoken and only need a written heads-up:

```
Hi [Name] — quick note: I am nominating [Project name] for Project Contractor
of the Year 2027. I will list you as customer representative. PBF will email you
a unique evaluation link to [work email]. Please watch for it (may look unfamiliar).
Due 31 Oct. I cannot fill it in for you. OK to use that address? Thanks — [Your name]
```

## Notes for platform upload

When you add this to the nominee dashboard (Wix), a **downloadable Word or PDF**
is easier for nominators than a long on-page block. Keep the full template above
as the source; the site can link to it or offer a one-click download.
