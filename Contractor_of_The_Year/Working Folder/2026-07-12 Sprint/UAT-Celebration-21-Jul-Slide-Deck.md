## Slide 5 — Participant reflections

**Title:** Your turn — in your own words  
**Presenter:** Facilitator (Peter) · unscripted (recorded with consent)

**On slide — pick one to answer:**

- In **one sentence**, how would you describe the Project Contractor of the Year Award to a colleague?
- What **surprised** you about taking part in the UAT?
- What would you **tell a contractor or client** thinking about nominating in September?
- Why does an award like this **matter** for the project business profession?

**Footer:** ~1–2 min each · these become quotes we can share (with your OK) · say so if you'd rather not be quoted

**Why these questions (facilitator note — not on slide):**

Each prompt is written so the answer stands alone as a **marketing testimonial** — a LinkedIn quote card, a website testimonial, or a launch-campaign sound bite. Steer speakers toward short, self-contained statements ("The award is…", "What surprised me was…") rather than inside-baseball UAT detail.

**Reuse plan for the answers:**

- **LinkedIn quote cards** — name + role + one-line quote over PCOTY brand (see [style guide](Award-Site-Style-Guide.md))
- **Website / homepage testimonials** — "What our first testers say"
- **Launch campaign sound bites** — short video cuts from the recording (see Appendix B)
- **Assessor & nominee recruitment** — social proof in Sep outreach

**Speaker notes:**

> "This is the interactive part and, honestly, the most valuable for us. Two or three of you — more if time allows. Answer any one of the questions on screen. We're recording, and with your permission we'd love to reuse a line or two on LinkedIn and the website as we build toward the September launch — just tell me if you'd prefer to stay off the record. Keep it short and it'll make a great quote."

**Consent capture (do after the call):** log who is OK to quote + attribution wording (name / role / anonymous). Confirm in writing before publishing.

**Time box:** ~8–10 minutes total
