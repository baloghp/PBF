## Nomination target & assessor capacity

**15 Jul decision:** **Do not publish a hard nomination limit** for cycle 1. The public constraint is the **1 Sep – 31 Oct 2026** window. Leila & Islam opposed a "first 100" cap; team agreed.

**Ops framing (internal only):** Recruit **50 assessor volunteers**, plan **~50% dropout** → **~25 active** at Stage 1. Internal planning may assume **~100 submissions / ~45 qualified** — these are **not rules for nominees**.

**Pre-vetting (Chris / Oliver):** Only **complete/qualified** nominations count toward any internal capacity tracking. Incomplete or rejected drafts must not block good nominations.

### Rule from governance (non-negotiable)

Every **eligible** nomination needs **≥2 independent assessors** (target **3** when capacity allows).  
→ Total scoring assignments = **2× to 3×** the number of **qualified** nominations ([Process Map](https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/117866524), [RACI](https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/117964816)).

### Recruitment vs active roster (50% dropout)

| Stage | Number | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Recruit (EOI + confirm)** | **50** | WS2 volunteer campaign — close **15 Oct** |
| **Expected dropout** | ~50% | No-show, COI recusal, capacity, calibration drop |
| **Active at Stage 1** | **20–25** | Who actually score in Oct–Nov |
| **Reserve / backup** | **+5** | On top of the 40–50 recruit target |

**Why this makes sense:** You don't plan recruitment to the minimum active number — you **recruit 2×** so dropout still leaves a viable roster. UAT converted **7** assessors from **16** volunteers (~44% usable in role).

### Capacity check — 45 qualified × 20 active assessors

| Active assessors | Assignments (2×45) | Noms per assessor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | 90 | **~4–5 each** |
| 25 | 90 | **~3–4 each** |

Comfortable load (UAT assessors scored **2** each; live cycle allows slightly more with calibration).

### Split the funnel

| Metric | Target | Drives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Submissions (internal)** | **~100** | WS3 comms planning — **not published** |
| **Qualified (internal ops plan)** | **~45** | Pre-vetted complete nominations — **not a public cap** |
| **Assessors recruited** | **50** (PBP or PMP proposed) | WS2 outreach (expect **~25 active**) |

**Eligibility (proposed):** assessors must hold **PBP® or PMP®** — see [Assessor Recruitment Strategy](https://ittd.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PBF/pages/263618649).

Intake drop-off (incomplete client form, eligibility): **~100 submitted → 40–50 qualified** is realistic (~50% pass rate).
