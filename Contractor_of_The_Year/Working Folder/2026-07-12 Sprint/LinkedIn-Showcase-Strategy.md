## 1. Purpose of this page

The Showcase is the **public face of PCOTY on LinkedIn** — not a second website, but a **discovery and trust hub** that:

1. Explains *why* Project Business contractors deserve recognition

3. Publishes the **comms cadence** (anticipation → launch → FOMO)  
4. Amplifies UAT volunteers, sponsors, and Oliver’s Project Business network

## 7. Content strategy by phase

Aligned with [07 Jul comms cadence](../../Confluence/Meetings/258637826 - Team Meeting - 07.07.2026.md):

| Phase | Dates | Goal | Post types | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Anticipation** | 15 Jul – 14 Aug | Build followers; educate | Welcome/pinned, Soban ✅, Leila industry (fixed images), Oliver thought piece, timeline graphic | 1–2 / week |
| **Launch** | 15 Aug – 15 Sep | Drive nominations | “Nominations open”, eligibility checklist, category explainer, assessor CTA, client role | 2–3 / week |
| **FOMO** | 15 Sep – 31 Oct | Close strong | Countdown posts, “last week”, social proof (UAT/volunteer quotes) | 1–2 / week |
| **Post-close** | Nov 2026+ | Maintain interest | Finalist teasers (when ready), sponsor thanks, behind-the-scenes | As needed |

### Content pillars (rotate)

1. **Why Project Business** — Oliver / programme voice  
2. **Eligibility & how to nominate** — Kris / Leila → always link award homepage  
3. **Industry stories** — Leila vertical posts (tech, health, …)  
4. **Community** — UAT volunteers, assessor recruitment  
5. **Social proof** — PBF, milestones, partner/sponsor (when signed)
