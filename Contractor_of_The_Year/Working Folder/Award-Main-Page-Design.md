### INTRODUCTION SECTION

**Heading:** About the Award

**Body Text:**
The Project Business Foundation (PBF) Contractor of the Year Award celebrates exceptional contractor-client projects that go beyond competence to achieve true excellence. This award recognizes contractors who not only delivered successful projects but created lasting impact—advancing their profession through innovation, demonstrating measurable transformation in their clients' organizations, and contributing knowledge that benefits the entire industry. Whether you're a solo practitioner or part of a large organization, if your project within the last two years exemplifies excellence beyond standard practice, impact beyond compliance, and legacy beyond project completion, we invite you to share your story and join a community committed to elevating the standards of project contracting.

### FAQ SECTION

**Heading:** Frequently Asked Questions

**Layout:** Expandable accordion

#### How much does it cost to apply?
Free. There is no application fee.

#### Can I submit multiple projects?
Yes, you can submit separate nominations for different projects, each with their own client assessment.

#### What if my project is still ongoing?
Ongoing projects are eligible as long as you can demonstrate substantial progress and outcomes to date.

#### Do I need my client's permission?
Yes. Your client must complete an assessment form as part of your submission.

#### What happens to my submission data?
All materials are retained for 1 year after the award cycle, then archived or deleted in accordance with GDPR.

#### Can I save my submission as a draft?
Yes. You can save and edit your submission before final submission.

#### What if I have questions during submission?
Your assigned Nominee Coach is your single point of contact for all questions and support.

## Design Guidelines for WIX Implementation

### Visual Style
- **Professional & Trustworthy:** Clean, modern design with professional imagery
- **Industry-Relevant:** Photos showing diverse project contexts (construction, tech, consulting, etc.)
- **People-Focused:** Show collaboration, client-contractor partnerships
- **Color Scheme:** Professional palette (blues, grays, accent color for CTAs)

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Bold, clear, hierarchy-driven
- **Body Text:** Readable, sufficient line spacing, max width for readability
- **CTAs:** High contrast, clear actionable text

### Navigation (Top Menu)
- Home
- About the Award
- Categories
- Eligibility
- Submission Package
- Process
- FAQ
- Submit Nomination (highlighted/button style)

### Mobile Responsiveness
- All sections must be mobile-optimized
- CTAs easily clickable on mobile
- Accordion/expandable sections for content-heavy areas
- Simplified navigation for mobile

### Accessibility
- Alt text for all images
- Sufficient color contrast
- Keyboard navigation support
- Screen reader compatibility
