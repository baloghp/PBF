## Overview

This document defines all roles required for the PBF Contractor of the Year program, including their responsibilities, accountabilities, and key activities. Roles are defined at the function level (not individual names) to accommodate a limited team where individuals may hold multiple roles.

---

## Executive and Strategic Roles

### Programme Owner

**Accountability:** Accountable for the overall success and outcomes of the PBF Contractor of the Year program

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Strategic oversight and decision-making authority for the program
- Final approval of winners and award decisions
- Approval of key policies (COI, data protection, appeals)
- Resource allocation and budget approval
- Stakeholder management at executive level
- Final deciding vote in case of stalemate (or delegates to designated authority)
- Program success metrics and reporting
- Risk management and issue escalation

**Key Activities:**
- Review and approve final award decisions
- Approve publication and ceremony plans
- Review program performance metrics
- Make strategic decisions on program direction
- Resolve escalated issues and conflicts

**Skills/Experience Required:**
- Senior leadership experience
- Strategic thinking and decision-making
- Understanding of award program governance
- Risk management

---

### Process Owner

**Accountability:** Accountable for the end-to-end operational workflow (refer to Process Map)

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Design, document, and maintain the complete process workflow
- Ensure process compliance and consistency
- Monitor process performance against SLAs
- Identify process improvements and implement changes
- Coordinate handoffs between phases
- Manage process documentation and training materials
- Ensure quality gates are met at each phase
- Process risk management

**Key Activities:**
- Maintain Process Map documentation
- Monitor SLA compliance
- Coordinate between Intake, Assessment, and Award phases
- Review and approve process changes
- Conduct process audits
- Provide process training to team members

**Skills/Experience Required:**
- Process design and improvement
- Project management
- Quality management
- Documentation and training

## Operational Leadership Roles

### Intake Lead

**Accountability:** Accountable for managing the intake and initial validation of all nominations

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Manage nomination submission process
- Conduct completeness checks on all submissions
- Perform eligibility screening
- Manage application lifecycle visibility
- Handle incomplete applications and follow-ups
- Respond to applicant questions within SLA
- Coordinate with Platform Administrator for system issues
- Assign eligible nominations to Stage 1 assessors
- Maintain intake metrics and reporting

**Key Activities:**
- Review submissions for completeness (within 24 hours)
- Verify eligibility criteria
- Update application status in platform
- Send notifications for incomplete applications
- Respond to applicant inquiries
- Assign nominations to assessors
- Track intake SLAs

**Skills/Experience Required:**
- Attention to detail
- Customer service
- Administrative coordination
- Understanding of eligibility criteria

---

### Assessment Lead

**Accountability:** Accountable for managing the assessment process (both Stage 1 and Stage 2)

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Manage Stage 1 volunteer screening and evaluation
- Assign nominations to assessors based on expertise and workload
- Coordinate Stage 2 judging panel activities
- Ensure COI checks and recusals are properly managed
- Monitor assessment quality and inter-rater reliability
- Manage shortlisting process
- Coordinate with Facilitator for Stage 2 presentations
- Ensure assessment SLAs are met
- Provide assessment training to volunteers and panel members

**Key Activities:**
- Assign nominations to assessors
- Review COI declarations
- Coordinate assessor assignments and workload
- Monitor assessment progress
- Review inter-rater reliability
- Manage shortlisting decisions
- Coordinate judging panel preparation
- Track assessment SLAs

**Skills/Experience Required:**
- Assessment and evaluation expertise
- People management
- Quality assurance
- Training and development

---

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Manage all communications with applicants (notifications, feedback, questions)

- Prepare and send award notifications (winners, finalists, non-finalists)
- Manage publication materials (case stories, press releases, social media)
- Coordinate award ceremony communications

**Key Activities:**
- Draft and send notification templates
- Prepare publication materials
- Coordinate with Programme Owner for approvals
- Manage embargo communications
- Prepare press releases and social media content
- Coordinate ceremony communications

**Skills/Experience Required:**
- Written and verbal communication

## Evaluation Roles

### Assessor (Volunteer - Stage 1)

**Responsibility:** Responsible for conducting Stage 1 screening and evaluation

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Review assigned nominations for eligibility and completeness
- Check for COI and report immediately if identified
- Apply Evaluation Framework (0-5 scale) independently
- Score all criteria with weights
- Provide per-criterion comments and evidence notes
- Consider contract type (fixed-price vs T&M) as applicable
- Complete assessments within SLA
- Participate in inter-rater reliability discussions if needed

**Key Activities:**
- Review nomination packages
- Complete COI declaration
- Score nominations using Evaluation Framework
- Document scoring rationale
- Submit scores and comments
- Participate in moderation discussions if needed

**Skills/Experience Required:**
- Technical, specialist, or compliance expertise
- Understanding of evaluation criteria
- Analytical thinking
- Objectivity and fairness

---

### Moderator

**Responsibility:** Responsible for moderating assessment scores and ensuring consistency

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Review inter-rater reliability across assessors
- Identify significant discrepancies (>2 points from median)
- Facilitate moderation discussions on outliers
- Calculate moderated average scores
- Ensure scoring consistency across categories
- Document moderation decisions

**Key Activities:**
- Compare scores from multiple assessors
- Flag discrepancies for review
- Facilitate moderation discussions
- Calculate final moderated scores
- Document moderation rationale

**Skills/Experience Required:**
- Statistical analysis
- Facilitation skills
- Understanding of evaluation framework
- Conflict resolution

---

### Judging Panel Member

**Responsibility:** Responsible for final evaluation and winner selection

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Attend Stage 2 presentations and Q&A sessions
- Independently score finalists using Evaluation Framework (0-5 scale)
- Ask questions during Q&A using standardized template
- Participate in outlier moderation discussions
- Participate in deliberation on winners
- Maintain objectivity and COI-free status
- Document scoring rationale

**Key Activities:**
- Attend panel briefings
- Review finalist materials
- Attend presentations (20 min + 15 min Q&A)
- Score presentations independently
- Participate in moderation discussions
- Participate in winner deliberation
- Submit scores and comments

**Skills/Experience Required:**
- High-profile expertise in relevant domains
- Evaluation and assessment skills
- Understanding of evaluation framework
- Objectivity and fairness
- Decision-making

---

### Facilitator

**Responsibility:** Responsible for facilitating Stage 2 judging panel presentations

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Manage presentation sessions (time tracking, introductions)
- Facilitate Q&A sessions using standardized question template
- Record responses during Q&A
- Manage recusals if COI arises during presentations
- Moderate outlier discussions
- Verify quorum before decisions
- Ensure decision-making protocol is followed
- Document presentation sessions

**Key Activities:**
- Coordinate presentation scheduling
- Brief panel members before sessions
- Manage presentation timing (20 min presentation, 15 min Q&A)
- Facilitate Q&A sessions
- Record presentation sessions (with consent)
- Moderate outlier discussions
- Verify quorum
- Document session outcomes

**Skills/Experience Required:**
- Facilitation skills
- Time management
- Meeting management
- Understanding of evaluation process

## External Roles

### Applicant / Nominee

**Responsibility:** Responsible for submitting complete nomination packages

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Complete written submission (≤2,000 words)
- Upload all required evidence artifacts
- Obtain and submit Client Assessment Form from client
- Provide accurate information
- Accept publication consent
- Respond to follow-up requests
- Prepare and deliver Stage 2 presentation (if shortlisted)

**Key Activities:**
- Access nomination platform
- Complete nomination form
- Upload evidence artifacts
- Coordinate with client for assessment form
- Submit nomination
- Respond to questions
- Prepare presentation (if shortlisted)

---

### Client Representative

**Responsibility:** Responsible for completing Client Assessment Form

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Complete Client Assessment Form accurately
- Rate 7 criteria (0-10 scale)
- Calculate scores correctly
- Provide evidence notes for ratings ≥8 or ≤4
- Sign and provide consent for evaluation and publication
- Upload signed PDF

**Key Activities:**
- Access Client Assessment Form (via QR code or link)
- Complete assessment form
- Calculate scores
- Sign and upload form

## Role Mapping to Process Phases

### 1.0 Submission and Intake
- **Responsible:** Applicant, Intake Lead
- **Accountable:** Intake Lead, Process Owner
- **Consulted:** Communications Lead, Platform Administrator
- **Informed:** Judging Panel, Programme Owner

### 2.0 Assessment - Stage 1
- **Responsible:** Assessors, Moderators
- **Accountable:** Assessment Lead
- **Consulted:** Intake Lead, Process Owner
- **Informed:** Applicants, Communications Lead, Judging Panel

### 2.0 Assessment - Stage 2
- **Responsible:** Judging Panel Members, Facilitator
- **Accountable:** Assessment Lead, Programme Owner
- **Consulted:** Assessment team, Facilitator, Program Secretariat
- **Informed:** Applicants, Communications Lead

### 3.0 Decision/Award
- **Responsible:** Communications Lead, Assessment team
- **Accountable:** Programme Owner
- **Consulted:** Assessment team, Judging Panel, Process Owner
- **Informed:** Applicants, Public

### Platform Setup (Pre-Program)
- **Responsible:** Platform Administrator
- **Accountable:** Process Owner, Programme Owner
- **Consulted:** Intake Lead, Assessment Lead, Communications Lead
- **Informed:** All team members

## Multi-Role Considerations

Given the limited team size, individuals may hold multiple roles. Key considerations:

1. **COI Management:** Individuals cannot assess nominations where they have conflicts
2. **Separation of Duties:** 
   - Assessment Lead should not be an Assessor for same nominations
   - Programme Owner should not be an Assessor
   - Process Owner can support but not make final decisions
3. **Workload Management:** Distribute roles to balance workload
4. **Skills Alignment:** Assign roles based on individual skills and expertise

**Recommended Role Combinations (if needed):**
- Process Owner + Intake Lead (operational focus)
- Assessment Lead + Moderator (evaluation focus)
- Communications Lead + Program Secretariat (administrative focus)
- Platform Administrator (standalone technical role)

## Notes for Role Assignment

1. **Start with Core Roles:** Assign Programme Owner, Process Owner, and Platform Administrator first
2. **Build Operational Team:** Add Intake Lead, Assessment Lead, Communications Lead
3. **Recruit Volunteers:** Recruit Assessors and Judging Panel Members
4. **Cross-Train:** Ensure backup coverage for critical roles
5. **Document Assignments:** Maintain a role assignment log
6. **Review Regularly:** Review role assignments and workload quarterly
