## Message (send to all)

Thank you for everything you’re putting into the UAT — it’s genuinely moving the Contractor of the Year Award forward.

We’d like to invite **one or two of you to join the core team**. This is the small group that helps **steer the program** — shaping the process, prioritising what to build and fix next, and keeping the plan moving.

### What it involves

- **A ~1-hour call, weekly or bi-weekly** (we’ll settle the cadence with whoever joins).
- Helping **move the plan along** between calls — light, focused input rather than heavy workload.
- A real say in **how the award and platform take shape**.

### Who it’s for

No special title needed — just someone who’s **engaged, reliable, and enjoys shaping things**. Whatever your current UAT role (nominee/client, coach, or assessor), you’re welcome to put your hand up. Joining the core team **doesn’t replace** your current UAT role; it’s in addition to it.

### Why join

- Direct influence on the award’s design and direction.
- A closer look behind the scenes of how the program runs.
- Great for your network and your own portfolio/experience.

### Interested?

Just **reply to this email** with a quick “I’m in” (or any questions). If more than two people are keen, that’s a wonderful problem to have — we’ll follow up individually.
